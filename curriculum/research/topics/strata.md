## 1. Scope and boundary

- Cleaning of common property in strata schemes and owners corporations: lobbies, stairs, lifts, bin rooms, car parks, pool and gym surrounds, and shared amenities.
- Shared lessons: kitchen and bathroom lessons extend to strata (DECISIONS.md).
- Out of scope: roof, facade and window work (window and roof courses), pool water treatment (pool course), waste-room sanitation beyond cleaning (UNCONFIRMED whether a separate course is needed), and any repair of common property.

## 2. Key methods and standards (Australia)

- Common-area cleaning schedule and method: contract specification and general practice. No Australian standard confirmed for strata cleaning. UNCONFIRMED.
- Owners corporation duties:
  - NSW: Strata Schemes Management Act 2015 (LISTED, legislation.nsw.gov.au Act 2015-50). The owners corporation's duties for common property are set in this Act. The exact cleaning wording was not read this pass: UNCONFIRMED.
  - Victoria: Owners Corporations Act 2006 (LISTED, legislation.vic.gov.au). Consumer Affairs Victoria guide to owning, managing and living in an owners corporation (LISTED). Cleaning duty wording: UNCONFIRMED.
  - Other states: UNCONFIRMED.
- Owners corporation rules can ban short-stay use in Victoria (LISTED; see short-stay brief).
- Chemicals, SDS and equipment: as residential brief. AS/NZS 3760 (LISTED).
- Waste rooms and bins: state waste and public-health rules. UNCONFIRMED.
- Pool areas: pool and spa course. Mention only if the pool surround is included in common-area scope.

## 3. Main hazards

- Slips on wet stairs, lobbies and car parks.
- Falls from ladders to clean light fittings, high walls and lobby glazing (see window and falls guidance, READ).
- Manual handling of bins and equipment.
- Chemical exposure in enclosed areas such as bin rooms and car parks. Ventilation and SDS required.
- Lifts and mechanical equipment: do not operate or enter lift pits. UNCONFIRMED state guidance.
- Electrical equipment in shared areas (AS/NZS 3760, LISTED).
- Biological hazards in bin rooms and shared amenities. UNCONFIRMED.
- Public safety: traffic and pedestrians in car parks. UNCONFIRMED per state.

## 4. Regulator references

- Work health and safety: Safe Work Australia model laws (READ, falls code page); state regulators (SafeWork NSW READ, WorkSafe Victoria READ, others as in residential brief).
- Strata law: NSW Fair Trading administers strata schemes legislation (UNCONFIRMED for this pass; the Act is LISTED). Victoria: Consumer Affairs Victoria (LISTED). Other states: UNCONFIRMED.
- Environmental and waste rules for bin rooms and washing areas: state environment authorities. UNCONFIRMED per state.

## 5. Typical scope boundaries

- Cleaning only. No repair, lift or plant access, or pool chemical handling unless the pool course applies.
- No decisions on owners corporation spending or service contracts. The cleaner advises; the owners corporation decides.
- Do not enter a unit or lot unless the contract and owners corporation allow it.
- Stop and refer where common property is damaged, unsafe or has a suspected hazard.
