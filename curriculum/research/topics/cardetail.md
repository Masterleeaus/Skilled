# Research brief: Car detailing

- Course slug: `cardetail`
- Group: Cleaning and hygiene
- Scope: Australia-wide (all states and territories). Victoria is not the default.
- Brief date: 2026-11-02
- Status: DRAFT. Reviewer status: NOT REVIEWED. Not verified, not published.
- Source key and UNCONFIRMED as defined in residential.md. Prior research: PASS37 car detailing research (PRIOR, 2026-10-23, Australian and overseas sources).

## 1. Scope and boundary

- Non-mechanical vehicle appearance care: wash, decontamination, interior cleaning, leather and fabric care, wheel and trim care, and condition records (per the prior research blueprint, PRIOR).
- Out of scope: spray painting, paint correction beyond the blueprint's conservative test protocol, repairs to live electrical systems, airbag or diagnostic work, hazardous remediation, and regulated vehicle repair.
- Mobile detailing (on-site jobs) raises wastewater and public-space questions; see section 4.

## 2. Key methods and standards (Australia)

- Wash and detail method (pre-rinse, contact wash, decontamination, drying, protection): industry practice. No Australian detailing standard was confirmed. UNCONFIRMED.
- Wastewater: EPA Victoria guidance on stormwater pollution from the auto industry (PRIOR; fetch refused this pass). The prior note: collect and manage wash wastewater, and do not discharge detergents or oils to stormwater.
- Stormwater and drains: NSW EPA stormwater page (PRIOR; not re-read this pass).
- Chemical selection, dilution and SDS: Safe Work Australia SDS page (PRIOR only). WorkSafe Victoria cleaning page (PRIOR; not re-read).
- Contact dermatitis and wet work: Safe Work Australia contact dermatitis overview (PRIOR; not re-read).
- Manual tasks in automotive workshops: SafeWork SA automotive hazardous manual tasks page (PRIOR; not re-read).
- Pressure equipment and hoses: detailing uses low-pressure equipment. High-pressure water jet rules are in the pressure cleaning brief. AS/NZS 4233.1 is named as the high-pressure water jet reference by WorkSafe Victoria (READ). Use it only if high-pressure equipment is used. Its application to detailing: UNCONFIRMED.
- Electrical equipment (vacuums, polishers, pressure washers, cords): AS/NZS 3760 (LISTED).
- Solvent and degreaser use in cabins: ventilation and SDS. Enclosed cabins are high exposure environments (PRIOR, UK HSE, not read this pass).

## 3. Main hazards

- Chemical exposure: concentrates, solvent-based products, degreasers, and cabin fumes.
- Skin damage from wet work and glove wear (contact dermatitis, PRIOR).
- Eye injury from splashes and product spray.
- Slips on wet forecourts and driveways.
- Musculoskeletal strain from kneeling, reaching and repetitive buffing (SafeWork SA page PRIOR).
- Vehicle movement in work areas.
- Electrical risk from wet equipment (AS/NZS 3760, LISTED).
- Stormwater pollution: environmental and regulatory risk.
- Fire risk from solvent-based products and rags (UNCONFIRMED specific guidance).

## 4. Regulator references

- Work health and safety: Safe Work Australia model laws; state regulators as in residential brief (READ, falls code page, for model status).
- Victoria: WorkSafe Victoria (READ) and EPA Victoria (PRIOR) for stormwater.
- NSW: SafeWork NSW (READ) and NSW EPA (PRIOR) for stormwater.
- SA: SafeWork SA (PRIOR).
- Trade waste and sewer discharge: local water authorities and trade waste rules. UNCONFIRMED per state.
- Consumer claims about products and coatings: ACCC and Australian Consumer Law. UNCONFIRMED.

## 5. Typical scope boundaries

- No paint correction or spray work in this course.
- No live electrical or airbag work. Refer to a qualified repairer.
- Do not discharge wash water to stormwater. Use a compliant containment, sewer or wash-facility route.
- Record existing damage before work. Do not claim the vehicle is restored or defect-free.
