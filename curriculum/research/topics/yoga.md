## 2. Regulatory position

Most yoga and Pilates rows are UNCONFIRMED. The matrix has no state licence for yoga or Pilates instructors in any jurisdiction read.

**Licence or registration**

- NSW: UNCONFIRMED at state level. Not checked in this pass (YOGA-NSW-01).
- QLD: UNCONFIRMED. Not checked in this pass (YOGA-QLD-01).

**Council permits for public land**
- NSW, Hornsby Shire: COUNCIL-SPECIFIC. Hornsby's commercial fitness permit lists yoga and tai chi among the permitted activities. This is one council only (NAT-YOGA-NSW-01). The permit conditions are described in pt.md section 2.
- VIC, Stonnington: UNCONFIRMED. The Stonnington permit lists personal training and similar activities but does not name yoga or Pilates. Each council must be checked (YOGA-VIC-02).
- SA, Holdfast Bay: UNCONFIRMED. Holdfast Bay's permit is for personal training. Whether it covers yoga or Pilates is not confirmed (NAT-YOGA-SA-01).
- QLD: UNCONFIRMED. No council permit confirmed.

**Working with children (only if children are clients)**
- VIC: CONFIRMED that the Working with Children Check categories for children's activities exist. Whether a given class falls within them is UNCONFIRMED (YOGA-VIC-03). See pt.md section 2 for category detail.
- Other states: UNCONFIRMED.

**Insurance**
- Workers compensation for employers: CONFIRMED in the states read. See hair.md section 2.
- Public liability: COUNCIL-SPECIFIC where a council permit requires it (see pt.md for the Stonnington, Hornsby and Holdfast Bay conditions). No state-level public liability rule was found for yoga or Pilates.

**Health screening**
- SECONDARY: industry screening tools exist (AUSactive pre-exercise screening). They are not statutory (YOGA-VIC-04).
- The Department of Health physical activity guidelines are cited in pt.md. They are general guidance and do not set screening rules.

## 5. Scope boundaries and licence notices

- The course covers general yoga and Pilates instruction for healthy adults and children. It does not cover physiotherapy, rehabilitation, injury treatment or medical claims. No source in the matrix supports them.
- Do not describe a yoga or Pilates certificate, a studio membership or a completed course as a licence, registration or accreditation. None was found in the sources read.
- Pilates-specific training is an industry matter. The national training package for fitness does not establish a Pilates qualification in the sources read. Do not cite a Pilates code until one is found.
- Council permits apply only in the named council. Lessons must say so.
