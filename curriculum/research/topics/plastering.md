## 1. Scope and boundaries

In scope:
- Dry-lining repair: plasterboard patch, joint taping and stopping, sanding, and making good.
- Cornice and ceiling patch work at handyman level.
- Skim coat and wall repair on sound substrate, using the products the lesson names.
- Preparation, dust control, and clean-up.

Out of scope or referred:
- Wet plastering, rendering and external render systems (licence and standard UNCONFIRMED; section 5).
- New dry-lining of a whole room or building (licence threshold rules apply; section 5).
- Fire-rated linings and fire-rated wall systems (refer to the specialist; standard UNCONFIRMED).
- Removal of asbestos-containing linings and jointing compounds (licensed removalist; section 5).
- Structural cracks, subsidence and damp (refer to a building surveyor or engineer).

## 2. Key technical methods

- Substrate assessment: check for sound backing, loose sheets, moisture, and any previous coatings or asbestos. Stop and test before disturbing older compounds.
- Plasterboard fixing: fix to framing at the spacing the product and standard require (AS/NZS 2589 listing; spacing UNCONFIRMED in this pass).
- Joints and corners: tape, bed, and feather coat in stages; allow each coat to dry as the product requires.
- Patching: cut back to sound material; prime; fill; allow cure; sand to a flat finish.
- Cornice: set out, cut, and fix to the backing; fill joints with the product instructions.
- Dust control: sanding dust control (wet sanding or extraction) to reduce silica and dust exposure; exposure limits not checked in this pass.
- Records: area, products, batch numbers, photos, and any finds (asbestos, damp) and referrals.

## 4. Main hazards

- Silica dust from sanding and cutting plaster and compounds (exposure limit UNCONFIRMED in this pass).
- Asbestos in older jointing compounds, textured coatings and sheeting (matrix thresholds in section 5).
- Lead in old paint beneath repairs (AS/NZS 4361.2 title).
- Falls from ladders, stilts and platforms.
- Manual handling of large sheets and bags.
- Eye injuries from dust and product splash.
- Electric shock or water damage from fixings into services.
- Slips from dust and spilled products.

## 5. Regulator references and licence facts

NSW:
- [NSW-02, CONFIRMED] Dry plastering (plasterboard, cornice, ceilings): contractor licence (individual or company) or qualified supervisor certificate for work over $5,000 in labour and materials, including GST. Qualification example CPC31220. Page updated 23 June 2026.
- Wet plastering and rendering: not covered on the page; UNCONFIRMED.

QLD:
- [QLD-02, PARTIAL] QBCC classes "Plastering drywall" and "Plastering solid" listed on the available-licences page (undated list). Building work valued over $3,300 needs a licence. Scope of each class not fully confirmed.

VIC:
- [VIC-03, CONFIRMED] Plastering-only jobs are exempt from BPC registration.
- [VIC-04, PARTIAL] Plastering combined with another registrable skill (for example painting) may need registration. The $10,000 threshold for combined jobs under that value is UNCONFIRMED.

WA:
- [WA-04, UNCONFIRMED] Plastering is not named on the painting scope page, the painter registration page, or the builder checklist. Do not infer a WA rule.

SA:
- [SA-02, UNCONFIRMED] Plastering not named on the FAQ or the contractor licence page.

TAS:
- [TAS-02, UNCONFIRMED] Plastering not named in readable sources.

ACT:
- [ACT-02, UNCONFIRMED] Not among the ten construction occupations in section 7 of the Act; not named on the planning page.

NT:
- [NT-02, UNCONFIRMED] No plastering class identified on the Building Practitioners Board page.

Asbestos (affects plastering, painting, tiling and demolition):
- [VIC-32, CONFIRMED] Unlicensed non-friable removal up to 10 m2 total and 1 hour in any seven days; OHS Regulations 2017.
- [QLD-10, CONFIRMED] Class A and B; 10 m2 threshold for Class B.
- [NSW-15, PARTIAL] Class A and B names; thresholds UNCONFIRMED.
- [WA-26, CONFIRMED] Class B above 10 m2 non-friable. [WA-27, UNCONFIRMED] unlicensed allowance not stated.
- [SA-19, SA-20, CONFIRMED] Class A friable and Class B above 10 m2.
- [TAS-19, CONFIRMED] Class B above 10 m2 non-friable.
- [NT-19, CONFIRMED] Class A friable any amount; Class B above 10 m2.

## 6. Scope points for licence notices

- NSW notice: dry plastering over $5,000 needs a contractor licence or supervisor certificate. The lesson does not grant it.
- VIC notice: plastering-only jobs are exempt from BPC registration; combined jobs may need registration (UNCONFIRMED threshold).
- Wet plastering and rendering: the lesson does not cover them, and the notice should say so.
- The lesson does not authorise asbestos removal. Stop and refer when suspected material appears.
