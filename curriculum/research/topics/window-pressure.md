# Window and pressure cleaning: official figures and rules
Checked 3 November 2026. Status: CONFIRMED / PARTIAL / UNCONFIRMED as stated.

## Work at height
- National model: no single height applies to all work at height. Construction work with a fall risk above 2 m is high risk construction work and needs a SWMS. Controls in order: ground-level work, fall prevention devices, work positioning, fall arrest, then administrative controls. No date shown. PARTIAL (model regulation definition not read). https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/hazards/working-heights
- NSW: general duty has no minimum height. SWMS required for construction work with a fall above 2 m. Only licensed scaffolders may work on scaffolds with a fall or falling-object risk above 4 m. Metadata modified 21 Oct 2026. CONFIRMED. https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/hazards-a-z/working-at-heights
- SA: threshold for high-risk construction work falls from 3 m to 2 m from 1 July 2026, under the WHS Regulations 2012. Page dated 5 June 2026. CONFIRMED (change); regulation text not read. https://www.safework.sa.gov.au/news-and-alerts/news/news/2025/lowering-height-threshold-will-raise-safety-standards
- Victoria: page checked gives no height threshold and no licence. Describes SWMS and the duty to manage falls. Published 1 Jul 2016; reviewed 27 Jul 2022. Vic threshold UNCONFIRMED. https://www.worksafe.vic.gov.au/resources/construction-safety-focus-preventing-falls-height-building-and-construction
- Queensland: no height threshold. Describes a fall from one level to another reasonably likely to cause injury. Notes a scaffold or EWP is safer than a ladder. No date shown. PARTIAL. https://www.worksafe.qld.gov.au/safety-and-prevention/hazards/workplace-hazards/agriculture/working-at-heights-and-elevating-work-platforms
- ACT: work where a fall exceeds 2 m is high risk construction work. Workers using fall arrest or travel restraint need competency-based training from a registered training organisation. No date shown. CONFIRMED (construction context). https://www.worksafe.act.gov.au/health-and-safety-portal/safety-by-industry/building-and-construction/working-at-heights
- WA: general duty to manage fall risk under the WHS (General) Regulations 2022. No threshold or licence stated. Copyright range 2017 to 2026 only. PARTIAL. https://worksafe.wa.gov.au/working-height
- Tasmania: code gives no threshold or licence; applies wherever falls are a hazard. Effective 5 Dec 2018; last updated 19 Sep 2024. PARTIAL. https://www.worksafe.tas.gov.au/topics/laws-and-compliance/codes-of-practice/cop-folder/managing-risk-falls-workplaces
- NT: not confirmed. UNCONFIRMED. https://worksafe.nt.gov.au/_resources/documents/pdf/codes-of-practice/construction-work.pdf
- Licence or training card for general work at height: none found in any state. ACT competency training applies to fall arrest users. PARTIAL.

## Licences and registration for window and pressure cleaning
- No licence or registration for either trade found on any regulator page. UNCONFIRMED across states.
- Queensland (council): pressure-cleaning wastewater is a prescribed water contaminant and must not enter gutters or stormwater. Cites an offence under the Environmental Protection Act 1994. Suggests capturing and vacuuming the water. Says nothing on detergents or permits. Council page last modified 22 Jul 2025. PARTIAL (council level, not state EPA). https://www.trc.qld.gov.au/water-pressure-cleaning/
- NSW, Victoria and other states: stormwater and detergent rules not checked. UNCONFIRMED.

## Ladders and elevating work platforms (EWP)
- National model: a high risk work licence is needed for a boom-type EWP where the boom is 11 m or more, measured from the original design. Boom EWPs under 11 m need no licence, but the PCBU must still train operators. Exceptions include supervised training, maintenance, moving without load, and loading or unloading. Scissor and mast lifts are outside this threshold. December 2025 model code. CONFIRMED (model). https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-12/model_cop_elevatingworkplatforms-december2025.pdf
- National model licence class list has no class for ladders. Lists the boom EWP class and scaffolding classes. No date shown. CONFIRMED (model list). https://safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/managing-health-and-safety/licences/high-risk-work-licence-classes
- Adoption of the 11 m threshold in each state: not confirmed. UNCONFIRMED.
