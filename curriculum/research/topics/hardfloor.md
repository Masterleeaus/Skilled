## 1. Scope and boundary

- Routine and periodic care of hard floors: tiles, vinyl, sealed concrete, laminate and sealed timber.
- Shared lessons: tile and grout sealing sits inside the TILING business (DECISIONS.md). Graffiti removal sits inside the cleaning group. This course does not duplicate them.
- Out of scope: floor installation, floating floors (tiling business), stone and terrazzo restoration (own course), timber sanding and refinishing (own course), and mould or flood remediation.

## 2. Key methods and standards (Australia)

- Floor-care method (dust control, scrub, rinse, dry, maintenance cycles, polishing and sealing): manufacturer instructions and general practice. No Australian standard for hard-floor cleaning methods was confirmed. UNCONFIRMED.
- Slip resistance: AS 4586 (slip resistance classification of new pedestrian surface materials). LISTED (codehub listing, 2013 edition). It classifies new surface materials. Whether it applies to in-service floors after cleaning or polishing, and how to test slip resistance after cleaning: UNCONFIRMED.
- Dust control on hard floors: wet methods are preferred over dry sweeping for dust-generating surfaces (WorkSafe Victoria, READ, wood dust guidance lists dry sweeping as dust-generating and recommends damping down). Apply silica controls where a site assessment requires them.
- Chemical selection, dilution and compatibility: manufacturer instructions and SDS. Safe Work Australia SDS page PRIOR only.
- Electrical equipment (floor machines, extraction units): AS/NZS 3760 (LISTED).
- Stripping and sealing chemicals (acrylic strippers, solvents): SDS and ventilation. UNCONFIRMED standard for application.

## 3. Main hazards

- Slips on wet and polished floors, and on freshly applied finishes. Post-clean warning signage and drying time.
- Chemical exposure from stripping and sealing products, including solvent-based products. Ventilation and PPE per SDS.
- Floor machine hazards: pinch points, cords, vibration and manual handling of machines.
- Dust from grinding, sanding and dry sweeping (see stone and timber briefs for silica and wood dust specifics).
- Falls from ladders when cleaning skirting and high surfaces: see window brief for height rules.
- Electrical risk in wet areas (AS/NZS 3760, LISTED).
- Unrecognised damage: cracked tiles, lifting vinyl or hazardous substances (asbestos-containing floor tiles and sheeting may be present in older buildings). Stop and refer; the asbestos code is READ for model status only.

## 4. Regulator references

- Work health and safety: Safe Work Australia model laws; state regulators as in residential brief.
- Asbestos in older floor coverings: Safe Work Australia Model Code of Practice: How to manage and control asbestos in the workplace (READ, model status). State licensing for asbestos removal: UNCONFIRMED per state.
- Electrical equipment: state electrical regulators. UNCONFIRMED per state.
- Wastewater from floor cleaning: state environmental authority and stormwater rules. UNCONFIRMED per state; the NSW EPA stormwater page is PRIOR only.

## 5. Typical scope boundaries

- No floor sanding, grinding or dust-generating restoration in this course. Refer to the stone and timber restoration briefs and their controls.
- Do not remove or disturb suspected asbestos floor coverings.
- Do not guarantee slip resistance outcomes.
- Stop and refer where a floor is damaged, lifting or unsafe.
