# Handyman: official figures and rules
Checked 3 November 2026. Status: CONFIRMED = read on the official page; PARTIAL = some elements confirmed or sources conflict; UNCONFIRMED = not verified.

## Licence thresholds
- NSW: contractor licence needed for minor trade work above $5,000 labour and materials (GST-inclusive). Page published 18 Sep 2024, updated 23 Jun 2026. CONFIRMED. https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/building-and-trade-licences-and-registrations/minor-trade-work
- VIC: registered builder needed for domestic building work above $10,000. Re-blocking, re-stumping and demolition need registration at any value. GST basis not stated. Page updated 25 Sep 2025 (VBA). PARTIAL. https://www.vba.vic.gov.au/consumers/home-renovation-essentials/engaging-builder
- QLD: licence needed for building work above $3,300. Work up to $3,300 needs no licence if it is not electrical or plumbing and no occupational licence applies. Owner-occupiers working on their own property are exempt up to $11,000. Page last reviewed Aug 2021 (QBCC). PARTIAL (labour, materials and GST not stated). https://qbcc.qld.gov.au/licences/start-your-career/when-you-need-licence
- WA: builder registration needed for building work of $20,000 or more in most of WA. No date shown. PARTIAL. https://www.consumerprotection.wa.gov.au/consumer-protection/hiring-tradespeople
- SA: licence threshold not confirmed. UNCONFIRMED.
- TAS: all low-risk, notifiable and permit building work must be done by a licensed builder. Owners or competent persons may do minor repairs on existing residences with no permit or licence. No dollar threshold confirmed. Guide dated 29 Nov 2016, so may be out of date. PARTIAL. https://www.cbos.tas.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0003/405039/Guide_to_the_Building_Act_2016.pdf
- ACT: licence needed for builders (including owner-builders), electricians, plumbers and gasfitters. Carpenters and bricklayers do not currently need one. No handyman threshold found. PARTIAL. https://www.planning.act.gov.au/professionals/regulation-and-responsibilities/construction-licences
- NT: prescribed residential work above $12,000 must be done by a registered builder under a signed contract (NT Government page). The Building Practitioners Board homepage states $25,000 for the same concept. CONFLICT, UNRESOLVED. Confirm with the BPB before publishing. https://nt.gov.au/property/building-and-development/building-and-renovating-a-home/what-is-prescribed-residential-building-work ; https://bpb.nt.gov.au/

## Boundary with licensed trades
- NSW: minor trade work is work that cannot be assigned to one trade. Endorsed licences cover specific items such as ducting, benchtops, splashbacks, shower screens, paving and bitumen. CONFIRMED (updated 23 Jun 2026). https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/building-and-trade-licences-and-registrations/minor-trade-work
- QLD: plumbing, drainage, gasfitting and some other trades need a licence at any value. Electrical work is regulated separately. CONFIRMED (page reviewed Aug 2021). https://qbcc.qld.gov.au/licences/start-your-career/when-you-need-licence
- VIC: standalone plastering, tiling, electrical, glazing, insulating, painting, plumbing, gas, drainage and floor-covering jobs do not trigger the major domestic building contract requirement. This is a contract rule, not a licence boundary. PARTIAL (updated 25 Sep 2025). https://consumer.vic.gov.au/licensing-and-registration/builders-and-tradespeople/running-your-business/domestic-building-contracts/what-work-requires-a-building-contract
- WA, SA, TAS, ACT, NT: boundary per trade not confirmed. UNCONFIRMED.

## Insurance
- NSW: home building compensation insurance required for residential work above $20,000 (GST-inclusive). Page updated 29 Apr 2026. CONFIRMED. Public liability: not mandatory per any source checked, UNCONFIRMED. https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/building-or-renovating-a-home/preparing/contracts
- VIC: domestic building insurance required for work above $16,000. GST basis not stated. PARTIAL. https://consumer.vic.gov.au/licensing-and-registration/builders-and-tradespeople/running-your-business/domestic-building-contracts/what-work-requires-a-building-contract
- QLD: home warranty insurance required for most residential work above $3,300, with labour, materials and GST counted. Some items need cover above that value. Updated 1 Aug 2024. CONFIRMED. https://www.qbcc.qld.gov.au/running-your-business/home-warranty-insurance-obligations/what-work-requires-insurance
- WA: home indemnity insurance required for building work of $20,000 or more. The certificate must be given before any deposit is paid. PARTIAL. https://www.consumerprotection.wa.gov.au/consumer-protection/hiring-tradespeople
- SA: building indemnity insurance required for domestic building work of $20,000 or more that needs development approval. Owner-builders do not need their own policy for their own work. Advisory Notice AN 03/25, November 2025. CONFIRMED. https://www.plan.sa.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0011/1558694/Advisory-Notice-Building-03-25-Building-Indemnity-Insurance.pdf
- TAS: 2016 guide does not mention insurance for residential work. UNCONFIRMED.
- ACT: home warranty threshold not found. UNCONFIRMED.
- NT: no insurance rule found. UNCONFIRMED.
- Federal public liability: not confirmed (business.gov.au page not fetched). UNCONFIRMED.

## Written contracts and quotes for small residential work
- WA: estimates are not firm prices. Extra work needs permission first. Deposits generally no more than 10 per cent. Quotes should show the trader's details, labour, materials, timing and possible extra costs. CONFIRMED (no date shown). https://www.consumerprotection.wa.gov.au/consumer-protection/hiring-tradespeople
- NSW: written contract required above $5,000. Small job contract from $5,000 to $20,000; large job contract above $20,000. Variations must be in writing and signed for large contracts. CONFIRMED (updated 29 Apr 2026). https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/building-or-renovating-a-home/preparing/contracts
- VIC: written contract recommended for all building work, including jobs of $10,000 or less. Major domestic building contract required above $10,000. CONFIRMED (updated 25 Sep 2025). https://consumer.vic.gov.au/licensing-and-registration/builders-and-tradespeople/running-your-business/domestic-building-contracts/what-work-requires-a-building-contract
- TAS: written contract for residential work above $20,000 (2016 guide). PARTIAL. https://www.cbos.tas.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0003/405039/Guide_to_the_Building_Act_2016.pdf
- NT: registered builder must sign a contract for prescribed work above $12,000 (see conflict above). PARTIAL.
- QLD, SA, ACT: written contract threshold not confirmed. UNCONFIRMED.
- Federal ACL quote and variation position: ACCC page not fetched. UNCONFIRMED.
