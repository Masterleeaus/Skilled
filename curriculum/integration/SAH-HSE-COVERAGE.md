## Coverage counts

## Conflicts (SA governs in SA facilities)

1. Sharps container fill point: SA three-quarters; HSE two-thirds.
2. Colour coding: SA optional; the HSE colour assignments differ from the SA examples.
3. Standard clean: SA requires it regardless of appearance; the HSE check-clean approach is not accepted for a scheduled SA standard clean.
4. Audit frequencies and acceptable levels: SA Table 2 (90 per cent high; 85 per cent moderate and low) versus the Irish frequency table.
5. Topping up of liquid containers: SA does not accept it; the HSE dispenser method refers to refilling.
6. Spill disinfection: SA says sodium hypochlorite is not routinely needed; the HSE manual prescribes a 10,000 ppm chlorine disinfectant. The strength-to-dilution link in the HSE text is inconsistent (see above).

## Errors found in the sources

- HSE audit worked example: room scores add to 420, not the printed 430; the average is 70 per cent, not 71 per cent.
- HSE spill note: 10,000 ppm is linked to a 1:10 household bleach dilution, which does not hold for typical household bleach.
- HSE corrective action time frame table: garbled in the uploaded text.
- HSE document name: the file is titled "HSE National Cleaning Standards Manual" but is the Irish manual for acute hospitals (2006).
