## Decisions

1. NHS coverage is 4 lessons, not more. The UK SOP is mostly governance and the operational content is already covered by existing lessons (see table).
2. CL2 coverage is 6 lessons, written only where the curriculum had no lesson or the source adds a step-level sequence (glass and mirrors, interior ladders, carpet extraction, ride-on sweeper, air blower, and the procedure layout lesson).
3. courseSlugs are exact folder names; checked against courses/. Healthcare lessons go to healthcare-cleaning, and commercial lessons to commercial-and-office-cleaning, as the business list in planning/DECISIONS.md states.
4. Module names: "Group (Cleaning & Hygiene): ..." follows the existing shared cleaning lessons. The healthcare names (Scope and authority, Shared: Growing and managing a team) and carpet names (Methods and technique) are taken from structure/COURSES-MODULES.json.
5. Layer is "group" for all ten lessons, because they are reused across cleaning businesses.
6. All ten lessons are lessonType K. None is a revenue or workload lesson, because the source material is procedure and governance.
7. The ride-on sweeper and the air blower lessons say that the lesson grants no authority to operate plant. The state licence or authorisation rule is UNCONFIRMED.
8. The schema's courses/<group>/REPORT.md is not written, because these lessons sit in shared/ and the list above is the report. Raise this if a group report is still required.
