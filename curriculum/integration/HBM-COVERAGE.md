## Decisions for review

1. Lesson type: the brief allows K or RH only. Content the catalogue would type as RV, W or C is typed K or RH. Repeat clients (HBM-11) is RH, and scheduling (HBM-16), lead generation (HBM-08) and tool staging (HBM-07) are K.
2. Course slugs: HBM-01 and HBM-07 are handyman only. The other 14 also list six property trade folders (deck-and-fence-maintenance, flat-pack-and-fixture-installation, locksmith-and-door-installation, painting, plastering, tiling) because the content is general to small trade businesses. Trim if the reviewer disagrees.
3. Source formula: the guide's labour-plus-materials divided by 0.50 is labelled a 50% markup but produces a 50% margin. HBM-14 teaches the correct markup and margin formulas and records the correction.
4. Figures: no US amounts, averages, deduction rules or named US services are used as current facts.
5. Assessment: passRule is NOT SET for all 16 lessons, because no pass standard for business content has been agreed. Quiz results are not presented as practical competence.
6. Rights: user-held recorded per brief. The PDF's own rights status is not stated.
