# Role pathways (draft)

Pathways group courses by the role someone plays. They are not learner routes. Each pathway takes the shared base modules listed, plus the service course(s) the learner chooses.

## Solo operator

Runs a business alone. Covers safety, job delivery, the business basics and marketing, plus the service course(s) they choose.

Shared modules:
- Professional induction and standards
- Workplace safety and risk
- Tools, equipment and maintenance
- Chemicals and materials
- Quality and job documentation
- Job management and service delivery
- Customer communication and privacy
- Starting a business
- Quoting and pricing
- Business operations and finance
- Marketing and sales

Service courses: Chosen by the learner (one or more)

## Team member

Works for an owner. Covers the safety, equipment, chemicals, quality, job and customer lessons they need on site, plus their service course. No business or pricing modules.

Shared modules:
- Professional induction and standards
- Workplace safety and risk
- Tools, equipment and maintenance
- Chemicals and materials
- Quality and job documentation
- Job management and service delivery
- Customer communication and privacy

Service courses: Chosen by the learner (one or more)

## Team owner

Runs a team of workers. Covers everything the solo operator takes, plus hiring, supervision, rostering and team management.

Shared modules:
- Professional induction and standards
- Workplace safety and risk
- Tools, equipment and maintenance
- Chemicals and materials
- Quality and job documentation
- Job management and service delivery
- Customer communication and privacy
- Starting a business
- Quoting and pricing
- Business operations and finance
- Marketing and sales
- Growing and managing a team

Service courses: Chosen by the learner (one or more)
