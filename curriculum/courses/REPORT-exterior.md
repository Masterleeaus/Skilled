## Issues for the reviewer

- Source bodies are cleaning-track drafts. Only the context section and the notices are exterior-specific. Method, access, equipment and site controls need a subject-matter review before release.
- Source drafts cite fictional or research pass IDs (for example premium-15, roof97-au-roofs) rather than URLs. They are kept in sourceReferences and need checking.
- Learner and assessor separation: some source files carry assessor-only expected responses (for example the high-access files). These were not copied into learner files. They should go to curriculum/assessments/.
- Shared lessons (the base and group items used on all four tracks) are written once per course, under courses/<slug>/. SCHEMA.md says shared lessons belong in curriculum/shared/ and must not be duplicated per course. I followed the brief path. Consolidation is a decision for the architect, and it would reduce the 345 files to 98 unique lessons.
- No prerequisites, business fields, or pass rules were set.
- Three files from another writer sit in courses/ (NEW-HAIR-01, NEW-HAIR-02, handyman-work-L01). They are not part of this group and were left unchanged.
