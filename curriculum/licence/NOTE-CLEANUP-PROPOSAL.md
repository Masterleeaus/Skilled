## Decisions (for review)

1. **Scope.** Only the 79 boom-group notes that begin with the scope-pass REVIEW text are listed. The other 8 boom-group entries have a null note, so there is nothing to replace.
2. **Form.** Each proposed note is one or two sentences: the current status and its reason, with the Tasmania gap, plus one lesson-specific sentence where needed. The old per-lesson topic summaries ("no licensed work taught" and similar) are dropped, because they described the old not_checked basis. Owner may want them kept; they can be restored from the current file.
3. **Tasmania.** Every proposed note says Tasmania is not set, because the apply record leaves TAS unset for all 83 lessons whose text states it.
4. **Three-state lessons.** `pressure-cleaning-L11`, `pressure-cleaning-L12` and `high-access-window-cleaning-L01` carry VIC, NSW and QLD only, so their notes say WA and NT are not set.
5. **Lesson-specific sentence.** A second sentence is added only where the old note held a point that still bears on the status (for example the licence-note point for high-access L01, L11, L12, and the superseded PV basis for F17-L04 and roof-cleaning-soft-washing-L09).
6. **Not changed.** 47 other entries (required or not_checked) carry scope-pass or "UNCONFIRMED" notes outside the boom group. Their status did not change, so their notes were not rewritten.
7. **Outside the 87.** The 11 boom-rule lessons in the apply record's "outside this task" list (for example property-maintenance-L04 and -L06, which are not_checked) were not applied and are not covered here. Their status needs an owner decision before a note can be fixed.
