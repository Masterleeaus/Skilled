## Decisions (for review)

1. NEW-AQU-04 and NEW-POND-02 changed to `threshold`. Reason: the NSW electrical page excludes electrical equipment operating at not more than 50 V AC or 120 V ripple-free DC, which is a threshold. These two had no other confirmed any-value basis.
2. Where another confirmed basis has no threshold (QBCC gas and plumbing, NSW plumbing, VIC PV, VIC WP, QLD locks), status stays `required`, and the NSW 50 V exclusion is added to the learner text instead.
3. "Last updated" rule: QBCC when-you-need page shows only "Last reviewed 11 Aug 2021" and is treated as current. The ESV gasfitter page shows "Reviewed 31 May 2026" and a "Date: 03/11/2026 6:19" stamp. The stamp is not labelled as an update, so 31 May 2026 is used as the update date.
4. Victorian gas wording: "always use a licensed gasfitter" is quoted from the ESV page. The ESV page does not say where gasfitting starts (meter or supply point), so that stays UNCONFIRMED.
5. Lesson text edited only where the official page or a fetched page contradicts or does not support it. Items I could not verify were left in place and marked UNCONFIRMED in the entry note.
6. Lesson-licence `limit` for NEW-BEA-01 and -04 changed: the claim that the mobile exemption excludes skin penetration is not on the Victorian page.
7. NEW-LAW-03/04 and garden-cleanups-L08/L09: the fact sheet confirms the exemptions (bush regeneration, on-land agricultural work, amenity horticulture). The 2018 currency is UNCONFIRMED.
8. handyman-work-L01: the QBCC $3,300 handyman exemption is confirmed (no electrical or plumbing, no occupational licence). The NSW $5,000 minor-trade figure was not re-fetched and is UNCONFIRMED.

## Conflicts found

- **garden-cleanups-L10 (Task B, resolved).** One scan group said friable asbestos needs a Class A licence at any quantity. Another said a 10 m2 / one-hour limit applied to unlicensed removal. Both are true for different material. Asbestos Victoria's removing-or-disposing page says "Only Class A license-holders can remove friable asbestos" and "A clearance certificate is required if the removal work involved any quantity of friable asbestos." Asbestos Victoria's unlicensed-removal page covers "the removal of non-friable asbestos-containing material" only, with the 10 m2 total and one-hour-in-seven-days limits. Split recorded in the lesson text and in the entry.
- **NEW-TIL-05 and other 10 m2 citations (Task B).** The Victorian unlicensed-removal page supports the 10 m2 / one-hour limit, for non-friable material only. The scan's phrase "cumulative across all employees" is not on the page and was removed from the NEW-TIL-05 entry. The 'employer or self-employed' wording was not quoted verbatim on the page.
- Lessons outside the 43 that cite the same Victorian limit (found by search, not reviewed in detail, not edited): PVB-08, construction-site-cleaning-L03, L11, C19-L02, handyman-work-L02, L09, L11, property-maintenance-L05, roof-cleaning-soft-washing-L03, L07, L12, CAR-NEW-04, NEW-TMB-01, NEW-PNT-04, NEW-PLS-03, HR04-mould-remediation, plus handyman-work-L09 and NEW-PNT-04 assessment files. Each should cite the unlicensed-removal page for non-friable material only.
- **Scan conflict on NEW-POND-02 / NEW-AQU-04.** Scan said "licensed whatever its cost"; the NSW page adds the 50 V / 120 V exclusion. Resolved to threshold.
- **Scan conflict on property-maintenance-L03 boom.** Scan gave NSW 11 m and QLD "not under 11 m". Verified: NSW WP applies at 11 m or more; QLD definition excludes platforms under 11 m. VIC has no length threshold.
- **Scan conflict on warehouse-deep-cleaning-L04 date.** Scan said "Published 2026-06-15". The forklift accordion page shows no visible date and its metadata is 6 Apr 2023. The parent page metadata is 15 Jun 2026.
