# Reclassification by scope (2026-11-03)

Scope pass over the 102 lessonIds that the earlier sync pass moved from not_checked to required or threshold. Each lesson was read for its own instruction, procedure and worked examples. The status was kept only where the lesson's own work falls under a confirmed rule with no threshold (required) or with a stated limit (threshold). Otherwise the default not_checked applies. Result: 71 required and 31 threshold moved to not_checked; no entry is required or threshold after this pass.
