## 5. Decisions (for review)

1. Food-processing sanitation lessons (FPS) placed in `commercial` only. DECISIONS.md lists food-processing sanitation under commercial and office cleaning. No pest-control or food-processing course exists in COURSES-MODULES.json, so no other slug is used.
2. Cleaning validation lessons (CPV): CPV-01 and CPV-07 are placed in `commercial`, because the reasoning and residue-removal checks apply to commercial kitchens and food areas. CPV-02, 03, 04, 05, 06 and 08 are formal pharmaceutical validation practice with no matching course, so courseSlugs is []. Each says so in its safety notes.
3. Module titles are taken from the commercial course list in COURSES-MODULES.json. CPV lessons with [] use "Shared: Quality and job documentation", which exists in that file.
4. Layers: high-risk for chemical, food-contact and pest-control steps (FPS-01 to FPS-07, CPV-04, CPV-06, CPV-07). group otherwise (FPS-08, CPV-01, 02, 03, 05, 08).
5. Pest-control licence notice set to true (FPS-05). FPS-06 also carries a note that building repairs needing a licensed trade are outside the lesson, with licence notice false.
6. Answer positions are rotated by the writer, so correct answers do not sit in one position. Learner files carry no correctIndex.
7. Prerequisites use existing lesson IDs only. All were checked against the shared folders.
8. Copy check applied to learner files and to assessor files. The brief asked for learner files; the assessor files were checked too, because their rationales are authored text.
9. The copy check was run with python3 -I, with SRC set to each source in turn, against FPS-, CPV-, and their assessment files. The learner and assessor files show zero 4-word overlaps against both sources.

## 7. Australian cross-check and conflicts

- Food Standards Code, Standard 3.2.2: listed in research/topics/commercial.md as PRIOR and not re-checked. Any application to contract cleaners is UNCONFIRMED. Not relied on in any lesson.
- Source priority: the Australian guidance already in the curriculum (C04-L01, HCG-08) states that the label governs concentration and contact time. Where the US source gives a figure (for example a hot-water sanitising temperature and time, FPS-04), the lesson states the figure as the US source's and marks the Australian requirement UNCONFIRMED. No Australian guidance was found that contradicts a source figure, because none was checked for that point.
- Sanitiser approval, phenol-based products in food areas, and allergen or residue limits in Australian premises are UNCONFIRMED.
