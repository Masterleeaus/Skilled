## 6. Decisions for review

1. Source classification: the team-leader book is treated as a reference compilation, not a course. The Total Cleaning manual is treated as a sample business document, used for sample practice only.
2. Course slugs for crew-facing team-leader lessons (TLD-01 to TLD-09) are residential, commercial, bond and shortstay, taken from COURSES-MODULES.json. The book is generic, so these slugs are a judgement about crew supervision.
3. TLD-10 (ethics and raising a concern) and TLD-11 (sustaining yourself) use courseSlugs []. No course fits these as course content, so they are shared base lessons.
4. Course slugs for THS lessons are commercial and shortstay, because the manual covers janitorial and housekeeping work. THS-05 (contract review) uses commercial only.
5. lessonType: all lessons are K except THS-05, which is RH. The reason is that the contract review checks price against staffing so that the hourly rate holds. This is a loose fit and is flagged for review.
6. Layer is base for every lesson. Module names are the canonical "Shared:" module titles from COURSES-MODULES.json.
7. Knowledge-check answers were rotated so the correct option is spread across positions A to D. The rotation is deterministic and built into the generator.
8. Source references use numbered or descriptive section names. Verbatim section headings were replaced to keep the copy rule. Document titles are kept as they are file names.
9. The Total Cleaning manual's staffing ratio (1.5 people per site), retention periods and dates are shown only as examples marked historical. They are not standards.
10. The ISO edition named in the Total Cleaning manual is not confirmed. No edition is stated as current.
11. Prerequisites: TLD-01 and THS-02 refer to F12-L03 (WHS duties and consultation), which exists in shared/team.
