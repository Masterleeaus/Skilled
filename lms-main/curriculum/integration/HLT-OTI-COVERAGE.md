## 3. Conflicts and resolutions (learner guide governs)

1. Terminology: the theatre document uses universal precautions; the learner guide uses standard precautions. Standard precautions used (OTI-01).
2. Droplet and airborne size figures: the theatre document gives them; the learner guide does not. Not used; UNCONFIRMED (OTI-01).
3. Sharps container fill: three-quarters full (theatre) versus do not overfill (guide). Facility fill line used; the fraction is not adopted (OTI-02).
4. Sharps transfer: a sharps dish (theatre) versus a puncture-resistant tray (guide). The guide equipment standard governs (OTI-02).
5. Wedding rings: allowed for all staff (theatre) versus a plain flat band only where the organisation permits (guide). The organisation policy governs (OTI-03).
6. Between-case clean: routine cleaning adequate (theatre) versus a defined between-patient wipe (guide). Guide method used (OTI-04).
7. Preliminary cleaning temperature: warm water (theatre) versus a ceiling of 35 degrees (guide). The guide figure is quoted and UNCONFIRMED (OTI-04).
8. Glove tear and wound: scrub solution for hands (theatre) and soap and water for wounds (guide). Each applies to its own body part (OTI-02).
9. Blood-borne virus disclosure: same care (theatre, silent on disclosure) versus confidentiality (guide). Both applied (OTI-01).
10. Waste rules: a UK trust waste policy (theatre) versus state and territory rules (guide). State and territory rules govern; the UK reference does not apply (OTI-05).
11. Learner guide edition: the guide cites 2019 Australian guidelines; the HCG lessons use the 2010 NHMRC edition. Not reconciled (UNCONFIRMED; recorded in HLT-01).
