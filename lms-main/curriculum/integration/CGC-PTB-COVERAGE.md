## Decisions for review

1. Source 1 is a Philippine competency module, not an Australian national unit. Used for practice content only.
2. CGC lessons have courseSlugs empty. The module label "no course slug assigned" is a placeholder for the main session to replace.
3. The bleach dilution, the AS and EPA references and the Act name in Source 1 were not used as facts.
4. Client-exclusion rules, cash finder fees, the 80:20 split and dollar prices were not adopted from Sources 3 and 4.
5. Sample figures in Source 5 are not used. PTB-09 and PTB-13 use hypothetical units only.
