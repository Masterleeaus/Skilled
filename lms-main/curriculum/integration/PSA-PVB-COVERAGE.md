## Existing pool lessons (not duplicated)

- pool-cleaning-L01 (scope): PSA-01 and PVB-01 add state-specific scope and definitions; classification itself not repeated.
- pool-cleaning-L02 (drowning and suction): PVB-07 adds Victorian suction rules; PSA-02 adds the SA clarity test. L02 left Victorian barrier rules UNCONFIRMED; PVB-02 to PVB-05 cover that for Victoria only.
- pool-cleaning-L04 (chemistry): L04 states it gives no numeric targets; PSA-03, PSA-04, PSA-07 supply SA values. Chemical separation remains in L04 and is cross-referenced.
- pool-cleaning-L06 (dosing and gas): PSA-12 covers the regulatory dosing requirement; gas handling cross-referenced to PSA-04. No new gas lesson.
- pool-cleaning-L07 (filtration diagnostics): PSA-12 supplies turnover limits; diagnostics left in L07.
- pool-cleaning-L08 (surface cleaning): PSA-08 adds the SA phenolic-product caution; L08 not changed.
- pool-cleaning-L09 (biological incident): PSA-10 and PSA-11 cross-reference the response steps; not repeated.
- pool-cleaning-L10 and L12: not overlapped by new lessons.
- Aquarium and pond course slugs: not added. The sources cover pools and spas only. The VBA note excludes fish ponds from the barrier rule (PVB-01); the pond course may wish to reference that later.
