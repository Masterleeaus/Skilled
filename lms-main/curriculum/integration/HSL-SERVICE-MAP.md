# HSL service map: handyman services list mapped to canonical courses

Source: List-of-Handyman-Services (cff95945-245696914-List-of-Handyman-Services.txt), a regional company's service list. Used as a catalogue only. It is not a course, and no lessons are written from it. Rights: user-held; reworded (no copying). No copyright notice appears in the document.

Course slugs are the canonical short slugs in structure/COURSES-MODULES.json (SLUG-MAP.json applied). Regional licensing is not covered here. Where a service is licensed trade work, the status says so and the correct action is referral.

## Status key

- COVERED: an existing course covers the service directly.
- PARTIAL: an existing course covers part of the service; the gap is named.
- REFERRAL: licensed trade work. Existing boundary lessons (handyman-work-L06, handyman-work-L07, additional-specialist-services-L04) say when to refer it. No course is needed unless the business holds the licence.
- NEW COURSE NEEDED: no course covers the service and the business may offer it.
- OUT OF SCOPE: no trade course in the catalogue; not mapped.

## Services that need a new course

These 20 services have no course and may be offered by the business: appliance installation (1), appliance repair (2), low-current systems (13), small masonry (20), building extensions (21), timber partitions (23), plasterboard partitions (24), water tank cleaning (34), duct cleaning (38), pest control (44 to 47), and air-conditioning service (56 to 62). Each is recorded here only. No lesson was written for any of them.

Several of these are licensed or regulated trades. For them, a new course would teach the work only, and the licence and registration rules are UNCONFIRMED. A business should not offer them without the licence the work requires.
