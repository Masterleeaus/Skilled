### HBM2 result

Source 4 is not a duplicate of HBM-01 to HBM-16. The HBM- lessons come from the Simply Business guide (dca133d1-Starting-a-Handyman-Business.pdf, 2017, US-based), which is a business guide. Source 4 is a UK do-it-yourself repair guide with a copyright notice, and its content is homeowner procedure. Its trade-boundary content is already covered by handyman-work-L01 to L07 and L10.

No HBM2- lessons were written. The only genuinely new material would be homeowner repair procedure, which is not business content for this curriculum. Nothing was copied from it.

## Decisions for review

- D1. Source 1 treated as a vendor sample (same watermark as source 2), so its rights are "not held by user".
- D2. No lessons from source 3, because a service list is a catalogue. The licensed items are mapped as referral, not taught.
- D3. Source 4 not treated as a duplicate of HBM-. No HBM2- lessons written.
- D4. Duplicates skipped (BP-01 structure, BP-03 SWOT, BP-04 positioning, BP-05 pricing, BP-06 cash timing, BP-07 roles and hiring, HBM-06 entity, HBM-03 start-up, MP-02 channels, MP-04 break-even, MP-08 milestones, SOL-05 outreach). The SMART-goal topic was skipped as it overlaps HBM-04.
- D5. Course slugs for BPX lessons: the seven property trade courses (handyman, deck, flatpack, locksmith, painting, plastering, tiling), the same set used by HBM-02 to HBM-16. The source is a handyman plan, so the wider cleaning courses were not included. The reviewer may widen the set, as BP-01 does.
- D6. Lesson type: K for BPX-01 to BPX-04, RH for BPX-05 (calculations and judgement).
- D7. Module: "Shared: Starting a business" for all five lessons.
- D8. Figures in worked examples are hypothetical, invented for teaching, and labelled as such. The sample figures are not used as facts.
- D9. The sample plans' internal inconsistencies (break-even figures, net margin percentages) are noted as a teaching point. No sample figure is reproduced.
- D10. Assessment passRule is set to the agreed text, "80% on knowledge checks; practical sign-off separate", for all five lessons.
