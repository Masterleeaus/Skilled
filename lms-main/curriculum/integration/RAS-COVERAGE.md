## 4. Decisions made (for review)

1. The 26 hazard rows are grouped by topic into 16 knowledge lessons. Each row appears in at least one lesson (see section 1).
2. The source has no blank form, so the template RAS-17 uses the register's column structure, the scoring scale checklist and the briefing record, with no sample rows or scores.
3. The template output format is "form" with six sections. All 14 fields appear in exactly one section.
4. The scoring scale is kept as the source's business scheme. It is labelled as a business scheme, not a legal formula.
5. The blanket spill-dilution rule (hazard 3) is not carried. The safety data sheet governs spill response.
6. Hazard 1 (coronavirus) is kept as a dated entry. Its isolation period is not carried, and current guidance is UNCONFIRMED.
7. Non-Australian standards (BS EN 166, BS EN 374-3, EN 388, ISO 20471:2013) are named only. SANS 51891 is not carried. Exposure action values taken from another jurisdiction are not carried as Australian values.
8. Licence notices are set on RAS-06 (elevated work authorisation) and RAS-08 (fixed wiring). Each states the lesson grants no licence and meets no duty.
9. Course slugs: commercial-and-office-cleaning and strata-and-common-area-cleaning on all lessons. Added healthcare-cleaning (RAS-09, RAS-10), high-risk (RAS-09, as the boundary for specialist body-fluid work), window-cleaning-all-heights (RAS-06), hard-floor-care (RAS-11) and residential-cleaning (RAS-04, RAS-07).
10. moduleTitle is "Shared: Workplace safety and risk", the module the existing shared/safety lessons use. No new module is created.
11. Answer positions in the knowledge checks were rotated after authoring to vary the correct index. Option wording and rationale were not changed.
12. No web check was run. All legal and standards claims are UNCONFIRMED.
