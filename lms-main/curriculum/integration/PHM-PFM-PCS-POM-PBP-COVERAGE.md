## 5. Decisions (for the main session to review)

1. courseSlugs: pool lessons use "pool". Business lessons use "pool", "residential" and "commercial", the base courses that fit. Aquarium and pond were not added: the pump, filter and chlorine material is pool-specific.
2. PCS lessons are in shared/pool because the instructions name that folder, with layer "business" and the shared business modules.
3. lessonType: K for all except PBP-03, which is RH (pricing for higher rates, per DECISIONS.md). RH and K are business categories there, not risk classes.
4. Layers: "high-risk" for chemical, electrical, pressure and drowning-prevention lessons. "group" for pool practice. "business" for PCS and PBP.
5. PBP-TPL-01 has no knowledge checks (schema for templates) despite the general instruction for three checks. It is recorded here.
6. Source conflicts (handbook, manual, textbook, guide): strainer interval (handbook weekly, manual more frequent): the more frequent interval is used. Backwash trigger: the manual for the unit governs. Chemistry targets: operator plan governs; no target is given.
7. Business plan figures are hypothetical and not used. Pricing is taught as a method with the business's own costs.
8. PHM lessons are for service workers who visit home pools; they are not homeowner instructions, and PHM-02 names what the owner must do.
9. Licence notices: set true for chemical, gas, electrical, heater, dangerous-goods, barrier-certification and business-registration content. No lesson grants a licence.
