Checked: 2026-11-02. Reporting only. No lesson, assessment or source file was edited.
Register: /home/claude/p29m/lms-main/curriculum/integration/FIGURES-REGISTER.csv

## Summary

- Lesson files scanned: 829 (learner lessons under curriculum/shared/ and curriculum/courses/). 378 lessons contain at least one figure.
- Figure rows in register: 888 (distinct lesson + figure). Occurrences before de-duplication: 1,256.
- Counts by category: UNSOURCED 364 (of which most are scenario or illustrative numbers), AU-RULE 249, GENERIC 114, FOREIGN 77, AU-PRACTICE 42, HISTORICAL 32, MANUFACTURER 10.
- FOREIGN rows with replacement_needed = yes: 77 (21 lessons). These are US, UK, Irish and other non-Australian figures. Per the coordinator's instruction they were not checked against foreign sources; a research agent is to find Australian replacements.
- Topics checked: 19 of about 38 Australian topics. Checked topics cover 19 distinct AU figures or groups; not-yet-checked topics are listed at the end.
- Status of checked topics: CONFIRMED 8, PARTIAL 9, UNCONFIRMED 2 (searches returned no page content). The other 19 are not yet checked.
- Most important conflicts:
  1. Engineered stone (NEW-STN-02): lesson says installed stone must be removed within twelve months of the ban. The Safe Work Australia page gives the 1 July 2024 start date but no removal timeframe. UNCONFIRMED.
  2. Wood dust and formaldehyde limits (NEW-TMB-04): the 1 December 2026 change is confirmed, but the Safe Work Australia page does not list the wood dust or formaldehyde values the lesson gives (5 and 10 mg/m3; 1 mg/m3; 1 ppm and 2 ppm). Values UNCONFIRMED.
  3. Overhead line and 11 m platform licence (11 m rule, 94 repeated rows): the QLD page confirms the 11 m boom-type definition but does not say whether shorter platforms need a licence. Lessons state that platforms under 11 m are "not covered"; this goes beyond what the page says. PARTIAL.
  4. Working at height SWMS (NEW-DEK / handyman / high-access): lessons state a SWMS is required where a fall could exceed 2 m. The NSW page gives "more than two metres" only as an example of high-risk work. PARTIAL.
  5. QBCC $3,300 threshold: the QBCC page confirms the figure but is undated (2023 copyright) and does not say whether labour and materials are included. PARTIAL.
  6. Victorian building practitioner registration ($10,000): the threshold is confirmed as the 2017 rule change (from 1 August 2017). Current status not verified. PARTIAL.
