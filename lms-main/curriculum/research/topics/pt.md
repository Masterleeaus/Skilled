Checked 3 November 2026. Status: CONFIRMED / PARTIAL / UNCONFIRMED as stated.

The regulations state they expire 30 June 2024; current status not confirmed. - Public liability and professional indemnity are commonly expected by venues and clients. Source is commercial broker guidance, not official. UNCONFIRMED as a legal requirement. No law requiring trainer insurance was found.

## Physical activity guidelines and screening
- Adults 18 to 64: aim for 150 to 300 minutes a week of moderate activity, or 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous activity, or an equivalent mix. Muscle-strengthening on at least 2 days a week. Breaking up long sitting is recommended. Department of Health and Aged Care fact sheet; no version shown; file path suggests April 2024. PARTIAL. https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-04/physical-activity-and-sedentary-behaviour-guidelines-adults-18-to-64-years-fact-sheet.docx
- Adult Pre-Exercise Screening Guide: Sports Medicine Australia released an updated edition on 31 Jul 2025. Does not state what it replaces. PARTIAL. https://sma.org.au/updated-pre-exercise-screening-guide-released/
- AUSactive APSS: older version found; not fetched. UNCONFIRMED. https://ausactive.org.au/membership/policies-guidelines-old/adult-pre-exercise-screening-system/
- ESSA pre-screening tool: found in search, not fetched. UNCONFIRMED. https://www.essa.org.au/Public/ABOUT_ESSA/Adult_Pre-Screening_Tool.aspx
- No Australian government-issued screening tool confirmed. UNCONFIRMED.

## First aid and duty of care
- Workplace first aid: the person conducting the business must provide first aid equipment and facilities and enough trained first aiders. Trained first aiders have no extra legal duties beyond other workers. Training should be refreshed. Safe Work Australia fact sheet, no date shown. PARTIAL (whether it applies to a self-employed trainer is not confirmed). https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/system/files/documents/1702/first_aiders_fact_sheet.pdf

## Consumer law: packages, memberships and cancellations
- National (NSW Government summary of ACL points): consumer guarantees apply; unfair terms can be voided by a court; misleading advertising is banned; cancellation should be in writing; direct debits may continue until notice is given. Modified 15 Apr 2026. CONFIRMED (general points). https://www.nsw.gov.au/legal-and-justice/consumer-rights-and-protection/services/fitness-and-gyms
- QLD: 48-hour cooling-off for new agreements. Cancellation in writing. Refund of prepaid fees within 14 days, less services used and any stated admin fee. Prepayment capped at 12 months. Ongoing agreements need written notice at least 2 months before the initial term ends. Ongoing minimum terms capped at 18 months. Page dated 23 Jul 2024; refers to a 2003 regulation whose status is not confirmed. PARTIAL. https://www.qld.gov.au/law/your-rights/consumer-rights-complaints-and-scams/buying-products-and-services/buying-services/using-a-fitness-service/join-a-gym-or-fitness-provider
- WA: 7-day cooling-off. Cancellation by written notice, effective on the day of receipt during cooling-off and otherwise within 30 days. Unused fees refunded within 7 days. Prepayment capped at 12 months. Agreements must be written, signed and dated. Regulation stated to expire 30 Jun 2024; current status UNCONFIRMED. PARTIAL. https://www.legislation.wa.gov.au/legislation/prod/filestore.nsf/FileURL/mrdoc_43289.pdf/$FILE/Fair%20Trading%20(Fitness%20Industry%20Code%20of%20Practice)%20Regulations%202020%20-%20%5B00-00-00%5D.pdf
- VIC: 2020 release says check cancellation terms and that Victorian law protects against unfair terms. Does not describe a cooling-off period. PARTIAL (older release). https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/exercise-your-rights-new-year
- SA, TAS, ACT, NT: not checked. UNCONFIRMED.
