## 1. Scope and boundary

- External and accessible cleaning of specialty appliances and machines: commercial dishwashers' exteriors, coffee machines, ice machines, commercial fryers' exteriors, washing machines and dryers' exteriors, and light-duty workshop machines, where the owner has authorised the work.
- Out of scope: gas appliance servicing and repair, internal component work, electrical repair, and any work that requires a gasfitter or electrician.
- Out of scope: refrigerant handling and air-conditioning service (UNCONFIRMED whether a separate course covers this; DECISIONS.md lists air-conditioning lessons in residential and commercial cleaning).

## 3. Main hazards

- Gas leaks and carbon monoxide when cleaning gas components or burners. Stop work and refer to a licensed gasfitter.
- Electrical shock from wet machines and damaged leads (AS/NZS 3760, LISTED).
- Moving parts and stored energy in machines (plant code, LISTED). Isolation before opening guards.
- Hot surfaces: fryers, dishwashers, ovens. Burns.
- Descaler and degreaser chemical exposure.
- Manual handling of heavy appliances.
- Water ingress into electrical components.

- Electrical: state electrical safety regulators. UNCONFIRMED per state (Energy Safe Victoria, READ, for PV only).
- Consumer and warranty: appliance warranty conditions may prohibit some cleaning methods. Manufacturer conditions: UNCONFIRMED per product.

## 5. Typical scope boundaries

- Exterior and accessible surfaces only, with owner authority.
- Do not open gas or electrical enclosures, or touch gas burners or igniters, unless licensed and authorised.
- Do not service, repair or re-commission an appliance. Do not reset safety devices.
- Completing the course does not grant any gas, electrical or refrigerant licence.
