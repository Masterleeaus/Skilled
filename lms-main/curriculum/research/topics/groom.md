- Course slug: `groom`
- Status: DRAFT research brief. Reviewer status: NOT REVIEWED. Not legal advice.
- Scope: Australia-wide. Victoria is not the default.
- Dates: all sources below were checked on 2026-11-02. Items marked "located only" were found by search and were not read.
- Matrix rows used: GROOM-VIC-*, NAT-GROOM-*, NAT-TRANS-* (for van transport), NAT-CHEM-01/02, ALL-VIC-02 in curriculum/research/personal/REGULATION-MATRIX-NATIONAL.csv.
- Note on the course name: DECISIONS.md lists "pet grooming". The matrix row name is "mobile pet grooming". This brief treats them as one business. Confirm the name before lesson authoring.

## 1. Business scope

- One business: pet grooming (DECISIONS.md). The matrix treats it as mobile grooming. Salon-based grooming is not separately covered in the matrix.
- Pet taxi and dog walking are in dogwalk.md. Boarding and sitting are in petsit.md.
- Grooming includes bathing, drying, brushing, clipping, nail care and ear cleaning. Medical or veterinary procedures are outside scope. The matrix contains no source supporting them.

## 2. Regulatory position

Pet grooming has the least evidence in the matrix. Most rows are UNCONFIRMED. The matrix found no licence or registration for groomers in any jurisdiction, but in most states this is because the rows were not checked, not because the answer is "none".

**Licence or registration**
- VIC: CONFIRMED that mobile pet grooming is not among the Domestic Animal Business types on Agriculture Victoria's list. Stonnington's list also omits it (GROOM-VIC-01). This is an absence from a list, not an exemption.
- VIC, council: UNCONFIRMED. No council registration for mobile groomers was confirmed. Councils should be checked (GROOM-VIC-02).
- NSW: UNCONFIRMED. Not checked in this pass (NAT-GROOM-NSW-01).
- QLD: UNCONFIRMED. Not checked in this pass (NAT-GROOM-QLD-01).
- WA: UNCONFIRMED. Not checked in this pass (NAT-GROOM-WA-01).
- SA: UNCONFIRMED. Burnside's dog registration does not name grooming (NAT-GROOM-SA-01).
- TAS: UNCONFIRMED. Not checked in this pass (NAT-GROOM-TAS-01).
- ACT: UNCONFIRMED. The ACT pet business page does not mention grooming (NAT-GROOM-ACT-01). The ACT page lists pet shops and boarding businesses only, so grooming is not on the licensed set; this is not confirmed as an exemption.
- NT: UNCONFIRMED. No grooming licence appears on the NT animal welfare page. Absence on one page only (NAT-GROOM-NT-01).

**Animal welfare duties**
- VIC: UNCONFIRMED. The Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act 1986 is the likely source. It was not read in this pass (GROOM-VIC-04).
- Other states: UNCONFIRMED. Animal welfare law was not read for grooming in any state.
- National standards: the national standards and guidelines page names no standard for dogs, cats or companion animals in the content read (agriculture.gov.au, checked 2026-11-02).

**Products and chemicals**
- VIC: UNCONFIRMED. No pet-grooming product rule found. General WorkSafe hazardous substances duties apply to employers (GROOM-VIC-03).
- National model WHS: CONFIRMED as a model position for the hazardous chemicals register and SDS. State adoption to confirm (NAT-CHEM-01).
- Veterinary products (flea, tick and parasite treatments): UNCONFIRMED whether any regulation limits who may apply them. No source read. Do not state a rule.

**Home and mobile visits**
- VIC: UNCONFIRMED. No rule found for groomers working at clients' homes. WorkSafe's self-employed duties may apply (GROOM-VIC-05).
- National: UNCONFIRMED whether a client's home is a workplace under WHS law (NAT-CHEM-02).
- Mobile van transport: UNCONFIRMED for groomers. The rules for transporting dogs in vehicles are separate. NT: dogs must be restrained in vehicles and trailers (NAT-TRANS-NT-01). ACT: the land transport code addresses livestock, not companion animals (NAT-TRANS-ACT-01).

**Insurance**
- Workers compensation for employers: CONFIRMED in the states read. See hair.md section 2.
- Public liability: UNCONFIRMED for grooming at state level.

## 5. Scope boundaries and licence notices

- Grooming is not a licensed activity in any state confirmed in the matrix. Do not state that one exists. Where a state rule is UNCONFIRMED, the lesson must say so.
- Do not teach veterinary procedures, medication, sedation or treatment of skin conditions. The sources do not support any of them.
- Do not imply that a grooming course gives a licence, a registration or authority to handle restraint drugs.
- Shampoo, dip and parasite products carry different rules. Only general SDS and register duties are sourced. Any product guidance must say the product label and SDS govern.
- Transport of a pet to the groomer belongs to dogwalk.md. Keep the boundary explicit in lesson text.
