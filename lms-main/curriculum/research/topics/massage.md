# Mobile massage: research brief

- Course slug: `massage`
- Status: DRAFT research brief. Reviewer status: NOT REVIEWED. Not legal advice.
- Scope: Australia-wide. Victoria is not the default.
- Dates: all sources below were checked on 2026-11-02. Items marked "located only" were found by search and were not read.
- Matrix rows used: MASS-*, NAT-MASS-*, NAT-ALL-*, NAT-CHEM-*, ALL-VIC-02, plus the VIC insurance and home-visit rows in REGULATION-MATRIX-NATIONAL.csv (curriculum/research/personal/).

## 1. Business scope

- One business: mobile massage, from DECISIONS.md.
- Massage is a named service in the Hair and Beauty Industry Award 2020 (MA000005), covering face, head and full-body massage. That is an employment coverage point, not a licensing point.
- Remedial or clinical massage claims, and any claim to treat a medical condition, are outside this brief. Nothing in the matrix supports them.

## 2. Regulatory position

Massage has the thinnest evidence in the matrix. Most rows are UNCONFIRMED. The team should treat that as an open scope gap, not as confirmation that massage is unregulated.

**Licence or registration**
- VIC: UNCONFIRMED. No state licence or premises registration for massage was found. The Department of Health's registration notices list beauty therapy, colonic irrigation, cosmetics, hairdressing, skin penetration and tattooing, and do not list massage. Boroondara's list also omits massage. The absence from lists is not proof that no requirement exists (MASS-VIC-01).
- VIC, council role: UNCONFIRMED. No council page specific to massage was checked. At least three councils should be checked (MASS-VIC-02).
- NSW: UNCONFIRMED. The NSW skin penetration page does not mention massage (MASS-NSW-01).
- QLD: CONFIRMED for the Gold Coast reading. Gold Coast's list of services that need no licence includes massage (NAT-MASS-QLD-01). Other councils and local laws were not checked. The Qld personal appearance page does not mention massage (MASS-QLD-01).
- ACT: CONFIRMED. The ACT page lists massage with hairdressing and eyelash extensions as other infection risk procedures. These need no infection control licence but must follow the Infection Control Code of Practice (NAT-MASS-ACT-01).
- SA: UNCONFIRMED. Massage is not named on the SA Health or Port Augusta pages. A council template covering massage was seen in search but not read (NAT-MASS-SA-01).
- TAS: UNCONFIRMED. Massage is not named on the reviewed Tasmanian pages (NAT-MASS-TAS-01).
- NT: UNCONFIRMED. Massage is not named in the NT registration list. Whether it counts as a body treatment is not confirmed (NAT-MASS-NT-01).
- WA: UNCONFIRMED. Not checked in this pass (NAT-MASS-WA-01).

**Mobile and home visits**
- VIC: UNCONFIRMED. No massage-specific home-visit rule was found. WorkSafe's self-employed and duty-to-others wording may apply in client homes (MASS-VIC-06).
- National: UNCONFIRMED whether a client's home is a workplace for WHS purposes (NAT-CHEM-02).

**Insurance**
- VIC: UNCONFIRMED for public liability. No requirement found for massage (MASS-VIC-05).
- Workers compensation for employers: CONFIRMED in each state read (see the hair brief, section 2, and NAT-ALL-* rows). Sole traders with no workers are not addressed clearly in several states.

**Products (oils, creams, lotions)**
- VIC: UNCONFIRMED. No product rule found for massage oils or creams. General WorkSafe hazardous substances duties apply to employers and workers (MASS-VIC-04).
- National model WHS: CONFIRMED as a model position for register and SDS duties (NAT-CHEM-01). Application to massage products is UNCONFIRMED.

**Client health screening and contraindications**
- VIC: UNCONFIRMED. The Department of Health publishes a massage client information sheet (updated 16 October 2021). Its content was not read, so contraindications and screening duties are not confirmed (MASS-VIC-03).

## 5. Scope boundaries and licence notices

- The course covers general massage for relaxation and wellbeing. It does not cover remedial, clinical or medical claims. Nothing read supports them.
- A lesson must not state that a client has a medical condition, or that massage treats one.
- Where a lesson refers to a state licence or registration that the matrix could not confirm, it must say UNCONFIRMED. Do not describe a licence as required.
- No licence for massage practitioners was found in the sources read. Do not imply that completing the course gives any licence, registration or authority to practise.
- If a council or state regime is confirmed later, add a licence notice naming what it covers.
