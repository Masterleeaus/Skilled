## Corrections and findings for the team

1. Solar accreditation: the CEC is no longer the accreditation body. Solar Accreditation Australia took over on 29 February 2024, and the CEC has ceased accreditation services (READ, CEC installers page). Any draft that cites CEC accreditation for solar work should be corrected.
2. Medical reprocessing: the ACSQHC advisory AS24/01 names AS 5369:2023 as the main national reprocessing reference, and sets the NSQHS (2021) Action 3.17 requirement (READ). Do not teach AS/NZS 4187 as the sole reference without checking its current status.
3. Engineered stone: the national ban on manufacture, supply, processing and installation took effect on 1 July 2024. Installed stone need not be removed, but work that removes or alters it should not be done (READ, SA Government). Import coverage is UNCONFIRMED. Cleaning and restoration drafts must not treat engineered stone as natural stone.
4. Victoria: the governing law is the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004, with the OHS Regulations 2017, and Victoria did not adopt the model WHS laws (READ). Teaching material should not describe Victoria as a model-law state.
5. Western Australia: the only source read describes the older OSH Act 1984 and is undated. The current WA position is UNCONFIRMED.
6. Model codes: the Safe Work Australia falls and asbestos codes are model codes. Their legal effect depends on each jurisdiction (READ).
7. Wood dust: WorkSafe Victoria (READ, reviewed 17 April 2026) gives exposure values by wood type and requires Dust Class M extraction. Check the values against the current national exposure-standard list before teaching them. A December 2026 scheme change is noted in the prior register but the link does not match its title, so it is unverified.
8. Carpet: AS/NZS 3733 is the named carpet maintenance standard, and a revised draft has been released for public comment (trade press, undated, READ). Its current edition is UNCONFIRMED.
9. Aged care: Standard 4 (The Environment), Outcome 4.2 covers infection prevention and control (READ). The Aged Care Act commencement date is UNCONFIRMED in this pass.
10. NDIS: registered providers may engage only workers with a Worker Screening Check. Clearances expire after five years, with ongoing monitoring (READ).
11. NSW short-stay: the code of conduct sets no cleaning standard and does not mention cleaners (READ).
12. NSW tenancy: tenants must keep the premises reasonably clean and leave them close to the starting condition, allowing for fair wear and tear (READ). The Victorian guideline text was not read.
13. Pressure cleaning: WorkSafe Victoria's alert (READ) says Class B operators must be trained and assessed through an RTO, with refreshers at least every two years.
14. Source quality in the prior drafts: several prior-register entries are US or UK pages presented as general references. One window-cleaning reference is a Victorian Department of Education policy page on falls prevention, which is not a window-cleaning reference. At least one prior URL (the Safe Work Australia exposure-limit item) does not match its listed title. Do not reuse these without checking.
