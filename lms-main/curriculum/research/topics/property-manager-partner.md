## Key rules for this brief

- This module is a partner module for trade businesses working for property managers. It does not make the trade business a property manager. Lessons must say so.
- The module grants no licence. Licences that apply to the trade (cleaning, gardening, handyman, locksmith and others) are set by the trade briefs.
- Module source lessons: C19-L01 (portfolio intake), C19-L02 (condition evidence), C19-L03 (vacate and tenancy scope), C19-L05 (service documentation), C19-L06 (agent handover), per `planning/CATALOGUE-V2.csv` (no C19-L04). `structure/COURSES-MODULES.md` lists the module as 15 lessons, 8 ready and 7 title only.
- Standards status: READ, LISTED, UNCONFIRMED (see the key in `pool.md`).

## 1. Scope and boundaries

In scope:
- Work orders from a property manager: intake, scope confirmation, access arrangements, and approvals.
- Pre-job and post-job condition records with dated photos, kept against the property checklist.
- Separate recording of pre-existing damage and new findings; factual notes only.
- Variation and approval workflow: quotes, approvals in writing, authority limits.
- Completion and handover: factual completion notes, rework plan, and invoice references.
- Escalation rules: stop work for suspected asbestos, unsafe structures, biohazard, or any restricted-work finding.
- Privacy-safe handling of tenant and owner details (see section 3).

Out of scope:
- Acting as a property manager, managing agent, or leasing agent. Tenancy decisions, lease notices, rent and bond matters, and tenancy advice (licence position per state in `portfolio.md` section 5).
- Entering a tenancy outside the agreed access arrangement and the tenant's notice rules (state tenancy rules UNCONFIRMED in this pass).
- Holding or paying out money for the property manager (trust money rules; see `portfolio.md`).
- Tenant communication beyond the job itself.
- Making legal or compliance statements about the property (for example, that a property is compliant with a standard) unless the trade holds the licence for that certification.

## 2. Key technical methods

- Intake: confirm property address, job, access method (recorded, not stored in plain text), contact, and approval limit in writing.
- Access: follow the property manager's written access arrangement; carry the job sheet; record arrival and departure times.
- Baseline photos: dated, from fixed positions, room by room, before any work.
- Scope note: state what is in and out of the job; sign or accept in writing where the system allows.
- Variations: stop, record, quote, and wait for written approval before extra work.
- Completion: photos after work; factual notes; list of items not done and why; referrals made.
- Handover: send the completion pack to the property manager through the agreed channel; keep copies.
- Records retention: UNCONFIRMED per state.

## 4. Main hazards

- Physical hazards in occupied and vacated rental properties: falls, trips, pets, children, and unsafe structures.
- Hazardous materials in older rentals: asbestos and lead (thresholds in the trade briefs).
- Water damage, mould and moisture: this module does not add mould remediation scope. Mould-related lessons sit in the pressure-cleaning and property-maintenance content, with warnings as DECISIONS.md states. The seven high-risk families remain one separate module ("High-risk cleaning").
- Personal safety when attending alone; refer any threatening situation.
- Privacy: tenant personal information in photos, notes and invoices.
- Key and access security: no keys or codes left in unsecured places.
- Disputes arising from unclear condition records.

## 5. Regulator references and licence facts

The module itself carries no licence. The licence position depends on the trade and state:

- Cleaning, gardening and handyman: see the trade briefs and `handyman.md` section 5. Gas, electrical and plumbing work are referred to licensed trades.
- Locksmith work (where the rekey or lock change is part of the job): see `locksmith.md` section 5 (NSW Class 2C; QLD security equipment installer; VIC UNCONFIRMED).
- Property management or letting activity: see `portfolio.md` section 5. NSW residential rental management: UNCONFIRMED. VIC estate agent licence (manage real estate for others): PARTIAL. QLD resident letting agent licence: PARTIAL.
- Asbestos and lead: trade briefs and `handyman.md` section 5.

## 6. Scope points for licence notices

- Every module lesson must say: this lesson describes how the trade business works with a property manager. It does not make the business a property manager and does not grant any licence.
- Where a lesson touches tenancy, the notice must say that tenancy and leasing decisions are for the licensed property manager or the owner, and that the trade business does not give tenancy advice.
- Completing the lesson does not grant a licence, certificate or authority.
