## 1. Scope and boundary

- Environmental cleaning of aged care homes, medical clinics, dental practices and NDIS-funded support settings: general and high-touch surfaces, patient rooms and common areas, waiting areas, and cleaning schedules.
- Medical clinic and aged care content sits inside this vertical (DECISIONS.md).
- Out of scope: clinical infection-control decisions; reprocessing or sterilising instruments (see medequip brief); handling waste and sharps as clinical duties; outbreak management decisions; personal care.
- Out of scope: any statement that the cleaner holds a clinical or infection-control qualification.

## 3. Main hazards

- Biological exposure: blood, body fluids, sharps and clinical waste. Stop and refer. Follow the facility's spill procedure.
- Chemical exposure from disinfectants (concentrates, mixing, ventilation). SDS and label duties.
- Cross-contamination between rooms and patients. Colour-coded cloths and zone sequencing per facility plan (PRIOR research).
- Slips on wet floors in corridors and rooms with patients.
- Manual handling of beds, equipment and bins.
- Aggression or distress from residents and patients (UNCONFIRMED state guidance).
- Lone working in residential aged care and NDIS homes (UNCONFIRMED state guidance).
- Electrical equipment (AS/NZS 3760, LISTED).

## 5. Typical scope boundaries

- Cleaning and environmental hygiene only, as written in the facility's cleaning specification.
- No clinical decisions, no instrument reprocessing, no sharps handling, and no resident or patient care.
- Outbreak protocols and the facility's infection-control lead prevail over general training.
- No certification that a facility meets the standards. Evidence records are not independent approval.
- Completing the course does not grant a clinical, screening, or NDIS authority.
