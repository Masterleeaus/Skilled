# Residential cleaning: official figures and rules
Checked 3 November 2026. Status: CONFIRMED / PARTIAL / UNCONFIRMED as stated.

## Work health and safety
- Model WHS laws adoption: Commonwealth, ACT, NSW, NT and Queensland from 1 Jan 2012; SA and Tasmania from 1 Jan 2013; WA from 31 Mar 2022. Each jurisdiction adopts amendments separately. No date shown on page. CONFIRMED. https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/law-and-regulation/legislation
- Victoria runs on the OHS Act 2004 (Vic), not the model WHS Act. Source: Safe Work Australia legislation page. PARTIAL (Act name not checked on WorkSafe Victoria).
- WA passed its own WHS Act 2020 (WA), operative 31 Mar 2022, which differs from the model in some areas. PARTIAL.
- Manual tasks: the duty is to manage the risk of musculoskeletal injury, starting with identifying hazardous tasks. Eliminate first, then mechanical aids, workstation changes, task rotation and training. No date shown. PARTIAL (no cleaning-specific text). https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/hazards/lifting-pushing-and-pulling-manual-tasks
- Working alone: the Safe Work Australia page covers agriculture and remote areas only. WorkSafe WA guidance note published 12 Feb 2014, last updated 11 Dec 2024, still cites the pre-2022 WA legislation. PARTIAL. https://worksafe.wa.gov.au/publications/guidance-note-working-alone
- Chemicals: manufacturers and importers must prepare an SDS for hazardous chemicals and review it at least every five years. Non-hazardous chemicals do not need one. Electronic SDS are allowed if a paper copy is available on request. CONFIRMED for supplier duties. Workers' access arrangements UNCONFIRMED. https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/hazards/chemicals/safety-data-sheets/preparing-safety-data-sheets
- Chemical risk duties (labelling, storage, hazardous chemical register): model Code of Practice page could not be read. UNCONFIRMED.

## Insurance and licences
- Public liability is not legally required for cleaners. Clients and contracts usually require proof of cover. Source is a commercial article, not an official source. PARTIAL.
- Workers' compensation is required for employers. Not verified against a regulator. UNCONFIRMED.
- No national cleaning licence. Local council, state or territory rules may still apply. PARTIAL.
- NSW: residential building licence may be needed for minor maintenance and cleaning when labour and materials exceed $5,000. The handyman research confirmed the same $5,000 threshold on the NSW Government minor trade work page (updated 23 Jun 2026). CONFIRMED for NSW trade work; cleaning-specific application UNCONFIRMED. https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/building-and-trade-licences-and-registrations/minor-trade-work
- Vic: labour hire licence may apply to commercial cleaning businesses with employees; residential cleaners not covered. Labour Hire Authority page not fetched. PARTIAL.

## Bond and end-of-lease cleaning
- Victoria: exit condition report must be completed within 10 days of the agreement ending. The renter must leave the property reasonably clean and in its original condition, allowing for fair wear and tear. For agreements from 29 Mar 2021, a landlord may require professional cleaning only where needed to return the property to its starting condition. Page last updated 14 Oct 2026 (Consumer Affairs Victoria). CONFIRMED. https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/housing/renting/rent-bond-bills-and-condition-reports/condition-reports
- NSW, Queensland, SA, WA, Tas, ACT, NT: tenancy cleaning rules not confirmed (pages blocked). UNCONFIRMED.
- Any state bond-cleaning standard: a private glossary mentioned a "REIA bond clean standards" term; not verified. UNCONFIRMED. Do not describe as an official standard.

## Consumer law: pricing and quotes
- Displayed prices must include all compulsory charges, taxes, levies and fees, as one total that stands out as much as any partial price. Optional extras such as delivery fees need not be included. Date shown 18 Jan 2024 (business.gov.au). CONFIRMED. https://business.gov.au/products-and-services/fair-trading/displaying-prices
- Component pricing: if a price is split into parts, a prominent single total covering all compulsory charges must also be shown (ACCC). No date shown. CONFIRMED. https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/small-business-toolkit/pricing-and-unfair-selling-practices/component-pricing
- Itemised quote requirement for services: not found on pages checked. UNCONFIRMED. Do not state as law.
- Misleading or deceptive conduct is prohibited under the Australian Consumer Law; ACCC page not read. UNCONFIRMED.
