## 1. Scope and boundaries

In scope:
- Wall and floor tiling in dry areas and in wet areas where the membrane and waterproofing are already in place and verified (see out-of-scope).
- Regrouting and grout repair; tile and grout sealing (product-specific).
- Floating floor installation (laminate, engineered timber, and vinyl plank) using the manufacturer's method and an underlay of the specified type.
- Basic tile replacement and repair at handyman level.

Out of scope or referred:
- Waterproofing membranes and wet-area construction (licence and standard UNCONFIRMED; refer to a licensed waterproofer or builder).
- Structural subfloor and slab repair; levelling compounds beyond the product's range (refer).
- Grinding or cutting concrete slabs at scale (silica controls; out of this course).
- Removal of asbestos-backed vinyl or mastic (licensed removalist; section 5).
- Electrical, plumbing and gas work (see `handyman.md`).

## 2. Key technical methods

- Substrate check: flatness, sound backing, moisture, previous adhesive, and any asbestos risk. Test before removing old tiles.
- Layout: set out from the room centre or the main line; check for cut sizes at edges.
- Adhesive and bed: choose the adhesive for the tile size, substrate and area; trowel size and bed coverage per the adhesive data sheet (numbers not checked in this pass).
- Movement joints: place joints where the substrate and the tile system require them (standard-based placement in AS 3958.1 listing; detail UNCONFIRMED).
- Cutting: use a wet saw or cutter with water and dust control; use an RCD-protected power supply.
- Grout: mix to the manufacturer's ratio; work in small areas; clean the face before it sets; allow cure.
- Sealing: select the sealer for the tile material and the grout type; test a small area first; apply and dry per the label.
- Floating floors: acclimatise the planks; use the specified underlay; keep expansion gaps at the perimeter and at fixed objects; lay in the order the system specifies.
- Records: area, products, batch numbers, layout sketch, photos, and any items referred.

## 4. Main hazards

- Silica dust from cutting, grinding and grout mixing (exposure limit UNCONFIRMED in this pass; controls: wet cutting, extraction, respiratory protection).
- Asbestos in older adhesives, mastics, floor tiles and asbestos-backed vinyl (matrix thresholds in section 5).
- Electric shock from wet saws and extension leads.
- Manual handling of large tiles, boxes of tiles, and bags of adhesive.
- Chemical exposure from adhesives, primers, sealers and solvents (SDS and label).
- Knee, back and wrist strain from long jobs.
- Slips on wet floors and tile offcuts.
- Water damage if a wet area membrane is damaged.

## 5. Regulator references and licence facts

NSW:
- [NSW-03, PARTIAL] Wall and floor tiling: contractor licence or supervisor certificate for work over $5,000 in labour and materials, including GST. Qualification example CPC31320. Floating floors, floor coverings, vinyl and timber flooring are not mentioned; UNCONFIRMED. Page updated 23 June 2026.

QLD:
- [QLD-03, PARTIAL] QBCC wall and floor tiling class, listed under "all other trade licences". Scope names ceramic, glass, marble, slate, stone and terracotta. Floating floors and vinyl not named (UNCONFIRMED). Terrazzo not named. Page last reviewed 31 August 2021 but refers to a 1 July 2026 fee increase; check the current version. Incidental work outside the class: only work valued under $3,300.

VIC:
- [VIC-05, CONFIRMED] Wall and floor tiling only: exempt from BPC registration.
- [VIC-06, UNCONFIRMED] Floating floor installation. The exemption list names "installing floor coverings"; the source does not say whether a floating floor counts. Confirm with BPC.

WA:
- [WA-05, PARTIAL] Builder registration for building work valued at $20,000 or more, where a building permit is required and the work is in the prescribed area (matrix wording). Tiling and floating floors are not named on the checklist, so it is not confirmed that tiling is builder work. A secondary search result (not a listed issuer) says registration is needed to contract on building work over $20,000; treat as corroboration only.
- [WA-06, PARTIAL] Tiler employed by a registered builder: a general builder registration rule, not a tiling rule.

SA:
- [SA-03, UNCONFIRMED] Tiling and floating floors not named on the FAQ or the contractor licence page. Whether tiling counts as building work is not confirmed.

TAS:
- [TAS-03, UNCONFIRMED] Trade class not confirmed; floating floors not named in readable sources.

ACT:
- [ACT-03, UNCONFIRMED] Tiling not among the ten construction occupations in section 7 of the Act; not named on the planning page.

NT:
- [NT-03, UNCONFIRMED] No trade class identified on the Building Practitioners Board page.

Asbestos (affects tiling, painting, plastering and demolition):
- [QLD-10, CONFIRMED] Class A (any friable; more than 10 m2 non-friable) and Class B.
- [VIC-32, CONFIRMED] Unlicensed non-friable removal up to 10 m2 and one hour in any seven days.
- [SA-19, CONFIRMED] Class A includes asbestos-backed vinyl floor coverings and other friable products.
- [SA-20, CONFIRMED] Class B above 10 m2 non-friable.
- [TAS-19, CONFIRMED] Class B above 10 m2 non-friable.
- [NT-19, CONFIRMED] Class A friable; Class B above 10 m2 non-friable.
- [NSW-15, PARTIAL] Class names confirmed; thresholds UNCONFIRMED.

## 6. Scope points for licence notices

- NSW notice: wall and floor tiling over $5,000 needs a contractor licence or supervisor certificate. Floating floors are not confirmed to be covered; say so.
- VIC notice: wall and floor tiling is exempt from BPC registration when it is the only trade; floating floors are UNCONFIRMED.
- QLD notice: QBCC wall and floor tiling class; $3,300 threshold; floating floors not named.
- WA notice: builder registration thresholds apply to contracted building work at $20,000; tiling is not confirmed to be covered.
- The lesson does not grant any licence. Asbestos-backed vinyl and old mastic are referred to a licensed removalist.
