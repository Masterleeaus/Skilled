# Research brief: Construction-site clean-up

- Course slug: `construction`
- Group: Cleaning and hygiene
- Scope: Australia-wide (all states and territories). Victoria is not the default.
- Brief date: 2026-11-02
- Status: DRAFT. Reviewer status: NOT REVIEWED. Not verified, not published.
- Source key and UNCONFIRMED as defined in residential.md. Prior research: construction-site-cleaning-research-pass19.json (PRIOR).

## 1. Scope and boundary

- Clean-up on active construction sites: removing waste, debris and dust between trades, and keeping access routes clear.
- The prior research defines the position: cleaning personnel are not authorised by course completion to remediate asbestos, lead, hazardous silica deposits or other regulated substances (PRIOR).
- Out of scope: asbestos removal, lead removal, silica remediation, demolition, and any work the site controller has not assigned.
- Post-construction final cleaning of finished buildings is a separate course (postcon).

## 2. Key methods and standards (Australia)

- Work within a site safety system: induction, site-specific safety management, permits, and traffic and pedestrian segregation. The Model Code of Practice: Construction work is PRIOR only (fetch refused). Its state adoption status: UNCONFIRMED.
- Falls and working at height: Safe Work Australia Model Code of Practice: Managing the risk of falls at workplaces (READ, model status). The SafeWork NSW construction page (READ) sets the control order: eliminate the need for height; use fall-prevention devices such as temporary platforms or edge protection; use work-positioning systems; use fall arrest only if prevention and positioning are not possible. Where a fall could exceed two metres, the page states a safe work method statement is required. Scaffold rules in NSW are in the same page (scaffold licence required above 4 metres, per the page).
- Silica: Safe Work Australia guidance on silica in construction (PRIOR; PDF fetch refused). Controls named in prior research: suppression and extraction, isolation, respiratory protection. Dry sweeping of silica-containing dust is restricted in overseas rules (PRIOR; UK and US examples, not Australian law). Australian state position: UNCONFIRMED.
- Engineered stone: national ban on manufacture, supply, processing and installation from 1 July 2024 (READ, SA Government page). Installed engineered stone need not be removed, but work that removes or alters it should not be done because it can release silica dust (READ). Import coverage: UNCONFIRMED.
- Asbestos: Safe Work Australia Model Code of Practice: How to manage and control asbestos in the workplace (READ, model status). Content not read this pass. Asbestos licensing by state: UNCONFIRMED.
- Respiratory protection: AS/NZS 1716 is named by WorkSafe Victoria for respirator equipment (READ, wood dust page). Fit testing requirements: UNCONFIRMED for the construction context.
- Electrical equipment on site: AS/NZS 3760 (LISTED). Electrical safety on construction sites: UNCONFIRMED.
- Waste classification and disposal: state environment protection rules. UNCONFIRMED.

## 3. Main hazards

- Respirable crystalline silica from concrete, masonry, stone and grinding dust.
- Asbestos in old materials disturbed during clean-up.
- Lead dust from older paint: UNCONFIRMED for construction-specific guidance this pass.
- Falls from edges, openings, voids and scaffolds (see falls code and NSW construction page, READ).
- Struck-by and mobile plant hazards: trucks, skip bins and loaders.
- Manual handling of debris and waste.
- Slips on wet clean-up water and debris.
- Electrical hazards from wet methods near live services.
- Unknown contaminated debris and sharps.

## 4. Regulator references

- National: Safe Work Australia (Model Code: Construction work; falls; asbestos; plant; READ or LISTED as stated above).
- NSW: SafeWork NSW (READ on construction page, which names two SafeWork NSW codes: managing the risk of falls at workplaces and managing the risk of falls in housing construction).
- Victoria: WorkSafe Victoria under the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (READ). Victorian construction module: LISTED (vic.gov.au SafeWork industry module).
- Queensland: WorkSafe Queensland (LISTED). SA: SafeWork SA (LISTED). WA and others: UNCONFIRMED.
- Silica and engineered stone: SA Government page (READ) on the national ban; state enforcement: UNCONFIRMED.

## 5. Typical scope boundaries

- Clean-up only. Do not remove, cut, grind or disturb suspected asbestos, lead, or regulated silica deposits. Stop work and report.
- No dry sweeping or compressed air on dust-generating surfaces unless a site assessment allows it: UNCONFIRMED per state; treat as a stop condition in training.
- No work at height, on scaffold or on roofs unless the site has approved that work and the worker holds any required training or licence: UNCONFIRMED per state.
- Course completion does not grant an asbestos, silica, scaffold, or high-risk work licence.
