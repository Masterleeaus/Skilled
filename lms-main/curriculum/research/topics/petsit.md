- Two models sit under this course: (a) sitting in the owner's home, and (b) home boarding in the sitter's own home. The legal position differs between them, and the matrix does not confirm either in most states. Treat both as UNCONFIRMED until the team sets scope.

## 2. Regulatory position

The most important finding is that the boarding definitions in the matrix tie "boarding" to a property other than the animal's normal home, or to premises and kennels. Whether a house sitter who stays in the owner's home is "boarding" is UNCONFIRMED in every jurisdiction read.

**Definition of boarding (VIC)**
- VIC: CONFIRMED. Boarding means caring for a dog or cat for fee or reward at a property other than the animal's normal place of residence. Every boarding establishment must register with the council as a Domestic Animal Business. Home boarding is recognised and limited to two animals from one household at a time (SIT-VIC-01).
- VIC, sitting in the owner's home: UNCONFIRMED. On the quoted definition, an animal staying in its own home is arguably not boarded. Whether any council treats it differently is not confirmed (SIT-VIC-03).
- VIC, Stonnington: CONFIRMED that the council lists for-profit homecare boarding among businesses needing a permit. The term is not defined on the page (SIT-VIC-02).

**Registration and licences by state**
- NSW: CONFIRMED that the Code No 5 for dogs and cats in animal boarding establishments names no licence. The code does not mention home-based minding or house sitting. No NSW licence for in-home sitting was found (NAT-SIT-NSW-01, NAT-SIT-NSW-02 UNCONFIRMED).
- QLD: COUNCIL-SPECIFIC. Several councils run kennel or cattery approvals and commercial animal premises licences (titles seen only). Whether pet sitting is covered is UNCONFIRMED. Business Queensland's list contains no companion animal boarding code (NAT-SIT-QLD-01, NAT-SIT-QLD-02).
- SA: COUNCIL-SPECIFIC. City of Burnside requires registration of a kennel business where dogs are bred, boarded or trained. It needs development approval first, runs for 12 months and needs microchipping, with exceptions. The text ties boarding to a kennel premises. In-home sitting is not described and is UNCONFIRMED for SA (NAT-SIT-SA-01, NAT-SIT-SA-02).
- WA: COUNCIL-SPECIFIC. City of South Perth requires a kennel establishment licence to operate a kennel or keep more dogs than the no-licence limit on premises. The page does not say whether it covers boarding other people's dogs or house sitting (NAT-SIT-WA-01, NAT-SIT-WA-02 UNCONFIRMED).
- TAS: UNCONFIRMED. Councils handle dogs under the Dog Control Act 2000. The FAQ does not say whether minding other people's dogs needs a kennel licence. Several councils list kennel licences (titles only) (NAT-SIT-TAS-01, NAT-SIT-TAS-02).
- ACT: CONFIRMED that businesses that board animals must be licensed under Part 3A of the Animal Welfare Act 1992. Licences last up to five years. Boarding is defined as daytime and overnight care (NAT-SIT-ACT-01). The ACT boarding code defines a boarding establishment as premises where other people's dogs, cats, puppies or kittens are cared for for a fee. Whether in-home minding falls within "premises" is UNCONFIRMED (NAT-SIT-ACT-02). Health checks and vaccination details: UNCONFIRMED (NAT-SIT-ACT-03).
- NT: UNCONFIRMED. The animal welfare page does not mention boarding, kennels or pet sitting. Council kennel licences exist (titles only) (NAT-SIT-NT-01, NAT-SIT-NT-02).

**Health, records and vaccination**
- VIC: CONFIRMED for boarding establishments. The Boarding Code requires a current vaccination certificate before admission, excludes animals suspected of infectious disease, requires a written arrangement with on-call vets, isolation facilities, and records of owner details, vaccination status and vet contact (SIT-VIC-05). Applicability to pet sitting: UNCONFIRMED.
- NSW: CONFIRMED for boarding establishments. Code No 5 sets daily checks, vaccination certificates dated within 12 months before admission, and record keeping (NAT-SIT-NSW-01). Applicability to sitting: UNCONFIRMED.
- ACT: UNCONFIRMED. The boarding code lists health care as an area; details were not read (NAT-SIT-ACT-03).

**Staff training**
- VIC: CONFIRMED for boarding establishments. The manager must be experienced and trained, and attendants trained for the animal type (SIT-VIC-06). Applicability to sitting: UNCONFIRMED.

**Insurance**
- VIC: CONFIRMED for boarding establishments. The Boarding Code requires a minimum of 10,000,000 dollars public liability cover (SIT-VIC-04). Applicability to pet or house sitting: UNCONFIRMED.
- Council kennel conditions in SA and ACT: not confirmed.
- Workers compensation for employers: CONFIRMED in the states read. See hair.md section 2.

**WHS for in-home work**
- UNCONFIRMED whether WHS duties apply in a client's home. WorkSafe Victoria's self-employed duties may apply (SIT-VIC-07). Safe Work Australia's working-from-home page covers workers at home, not client homes (NAT-CHEM-02).

**Award coverage (employees)**
- The Fair Work animal attendants page names three awards that cover animal attendants, depending on the employer. The Miscellaneous Award is named for pet boarding businesses. Pet sitters are not named. Whether a house sitter employee falls under a given award is UNCONFIRMED (K600594, checked 2026-11-02).

## 5. Scope boundaries and licence notices

- Licence notice (required): a lesson that covers boarding, kennel or cattery work must say which licence applies, the state or council that issues it, and that the lesson does not grant a licence. In the ACT, boarding is licensed under Part 3A (NAT-SIT-ACT-01). Elsewhere the position is council-specific or UNCONFIRMED.
- Do not describe house sitting as boarding, or as not boarding, in any lesson. Use the matrix wording: UNCONFIRMED.
- Home boarding in the sitter's own home is limited in Victoria to two animals from one household at a time (SIT-VIC-01). State this only as the Victorian position.
- Do not imply that the Boarding Code's requirements, such as the 10 million dollar cover, apply to house sitting. They apply to boarding establishments and their applicability is UNCONFIRMED.
- Grooming in the client's home belongs to groom.md.
