## 1. Scope and boundaries

In scope:
- Cleaning and servicing of pond filters, pumps accessible for service, skimmers, UV units (power off and isolated first), and pre-filters.
- Removal of debris, leaves, sludge and algae from the pond and surrounds.
- Water-quality observation and records (visual, test-kit readings).
- Plant care: trimming, removing dead material, and fitting baskets.
- Visual checks of edges, liner and fittings for leaks or damage (report, do not repair).
- Client advice on feeding, stocking limits and biosecurity.

Out of scope or referred:
- Liner repair, pond excavation, new construction, and any structural work.
- Electrical connection, wiring, and mains-powered equipment (licensed electrician).
- New or altered plumbing, pipe joins, or outlet changes (licensed plumber; see section 5).
- Algaecide or other pesticide use for hire unless the state rule allows it (see `lawn.md` section 5 for the NSW pesticide licences).
- Water extraction from natural waterways, dams or bores: UNCONFIRMED in this pass.
- Catching, moving, releasing or selling fish. Any suspected prohibited or restricted fish must be reported (section 5).

## 2. Key technical methods

- Isolate power before opening pumps, skimmers or UV units. Confirm isolation with a test before handling.
- Filter service: remove media, rinse in pond water (not tap water) to keep beneficial bacteria; reassemble and check flow.
- Sediment removal: use a method that limits fish stress and water loss; record volume removed.
- Water checks: visual clarity, surface film, odour, algae type, and simple test-kit readings where the client asks for them. Target ranges UNCONFIRMED in this pass.
- Chlorinated tap water: dechlorinate before adding to the pond. The method is a technique point; no source checked in this pass.
- Liner check: visual and tactile check of edges, folds and joins; record the location of any leak signs.
- Plant care: remove dead matter, prevent over-stocking of floating plants.
- Records: service date, parts replaced, readings, fish numbers as the client reports them, and client decisions.

## 4. Main hazards

- Electric shock from pumps, lights and cords near water (QLD ESO guidance). Cords and extension leads must be kept out of water; plug-in equipment must not be placed where it could fall in.
- Drowning, especially for small children; pond depth and edges.
- Slips and falls on wet rocks, edging and liner folds.
- Manual handling of rocks, bags of media, and sludge buckets.
- Biosecurity: moving water, fish, plants or sludge between sites can spread pest species. Queensland and NSW rules apply (section 5).
- Pesticides and algaecides: skin and eye contact, drift, storage. Use label directions (see `lawn.md`).
- Wildlife and insects: specific hazards UNCONFIRMED in this pass.

## 5. Regulator references and licence facts

Licence facts for ponds: none in the matrices. Use these rules instead, noting their status.

- QLD (invasive fish; Queensland page last updated 28 May 2026, read): Prohibited noxious fish are not in Queensland and cannot be brought in. Sightings must be reported to Biosecurity Queensland within 24 hours. Restricted fish cannot be kept, fed, given away, sold or released. Owners must take reasonable, practical steps to stop escape and spread.
- NSW (aquarium and pond fish, page undated, read): Releasing fish into NSW public waters needs a fish stocking permit under the Fisheries Management Act. Dumping an unwanted fish is an offence under the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act 1979. Prohibited and notifiable species are listed in Schedule 2 of the Biosecurity Act and Schedule 1 of the Biosecurity Regulation. The NSW page does not state the Biosecurity Act year; confirm which Act applies.
- Electrical: QLD ESO page says a licensed electrician must install and maintain pool wiring and electrical equipment, and that regular maintenance should also be done by a licensed electrician. NSW wiring work needs an electrical contractor licence holder regardless of cost [NSW-07, CONFIRMED]. QLD electrical rules are in [QLD-07, PARTIAL]. Pond-specific application UNCONFIRMED.
- Plumbing: NSW plumbing and water plumbing (including urban irrigation) need a licence or certificate regardless of cost [NSW-06, CONFIRMED]. Whether pond pipework is "water plumbing" is UNCONFIRMED. QLD plumbing needs a QBCC licence at any value [QLD-06, CONFIRMED]. VIC gasfitting and drainage classes [VIC-27, VIC-30] are PARTIAL or CONFIRMED as stated in the matrix. Other states: UNCONFIRMED.
- Water extraction, dams and bores: UNCONFIRMED in this pass.
