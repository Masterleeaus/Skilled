## 1. Scope and boundary

- Low-pressure soft washing of roof surfaces, gutters, roof edges and related exterior surfaces, where roof access is approved and safe.
- Prior research safety boundaries (PRIOR): avoid roof access where practicable; presume roof fragility until competent confirmation; protect openings and skylights; no unapproved chemicals, pressure settings or uncontrolled runoff; no compliance, roof-integrity or remediation-clearance certification.
- Out of scope: roof repair, re-sheeting, asbestos sheeting removal, roof-integrity assessment, solar panel cleaning (solar course), and high-pressure roof jetting.

## 2. Key methods and standards (Australia)

- Work-at-height hierarchy (READ, SafeWork NSW construction page): eliminate the need for height; physical barriers such as edge protection or scaffolding; work-positioning and fall arrest only where others are not possible. For roofs, the page calls for physical barriers, skylight covers, crawl boards and marked exclusion zones around fragile surfaces.
- Model Code of Practice: Managing the risk of falls at workplaces (READ, model status; legal effect by jurisdiction).
- Roof work guidance: Safe Work Australia information sheet "Safe work on roofs" (LISTED; search result, not read).
- Fragile roofing guidance: SafeWork SA fragile roofing page (LISTED); WorkSafe Victoria falls through skylights, fragile roofs, voids and penetrations (PRIOR; listed, not read); WA WorkSafe health and safety bulletin on falls through fragile roofs and roof openings (LISTED, search result; not read).
- Asbestos in roof sheeting: Safe Work Australia Model Code: How to manage and control asbestos in the workplace (READ, model status). Old roof sheeting may contain asbestos; the specific Australian guidance on roof sheeting: UNCONFIRMED this pass.
- Solar panels on the roof: AS/NZS 5033 (LISTED; 2021 edition in a store listing). See solar brief.
- Chemicals (soft-wash mixes, biocides, surfactants): SDS and labels. The Safe Work Australia SDS page is PRIOR only. Product, concentration and runoff limits: UNCONFIRMED.
- Stormwater and vegetation: NSW EPA stormwater guidance (PRIOR; not re-read). Local council and stormwater rules: UNCONFIRMED per council.
- Electrical services near the roof: state electrical rules: UNCONFIRMED per state.

## 3. Main hazards

- Falls through fragile sheeting, skylights, voids and penetrations (the leading hazard).
- Falls from edges and ladders.
- Slips on wet and mossy surfaces.
- Chemical exposure and splash.
- Falling debris and tools onto people and property below. Exclusion zones.
- Electrical contact with overhead services and roof-mounted solar arrays (see solar brief).
- Asbestos-containing sheeting: disturbance by washing or walking.
- Weather: wind, rain and heat. Stop-work triggers.
- Rescue after a fall: must be planned in advance (PRIOR research; state rules UNCONFIRMED).

## 5. Typical scope boundaries

- Ground-based alternatives first. Roof access only with approved edge protection or equipment, a documented plan, and competent oversight.
- No work on fragile or unknown roofing without confirmed competence, barriers and skylight protection.
- No roof-integrity, compliance or remediation-clearance statements. Report defects in writing.
- No removal or disturbance of asbestos-containing sheeting.
- No chemicals or pressure settings beyond approved methods.
- Completing the course does not grant a roof-work, height-work or licence authority.
