## 1. Scope and boundary

- External cleaning only of non-critical medical equipment and furniture in clinics and care settings: trolleys, bed frames, cabinets, monitors' casings, chairs and external surfaces of equipment, where the facility has authorised the work.
- Out of scope (prior research, consistent with the course title): reprocessing, disinfection or sterilisation of reusable medical devices; handling of invasive devices; any release of equipment to patient care; and clinical decisions.
- Out of scope: any equipment the facility has classed as critical or semi-critical unless the facility's own reprocessing team does the work.

## 2. Key methods and standards (Australia)

- National reprocessing standard: AS 5369:2023. READ via the ACSQHC advisory: the advisory names AS 5369:2023 as the main national standard for reprocessing reusable medical devices, alongside manufacturer instructions and the Australian Guidelines for the Prevention and Control of Infection in Healthcare. The advisory describes AS 5369:2023 as covering reprocessing of reusable medical devices and other devices in health and non-health related facilities. Use the standard's own text for any detail: not read this pass.
- Advisory AS24/01 (READ): applies to health service organisations and accrediting agencies. Dated 11 November 2024, version 1.0; replaces AS18/07. It sets compliance requirements under the NSQHS Standards (2021 edition), Preventing and Controlling Infections Standard, Action 3.17. The advisory's gap analysis deadline was 30 June 2025.

## 3. Main hazards

- Biological contamination on external surfaces (blood, body fluids). Stop, use the facility's spill procedure, and refer. Victorian Department of Health spill guidance is PRIOR only (not read).
- Sharps and needles left on or near equipment. Do not handle; report.
- Chemical exposure from disinfectants and cleaners. SDS and ventilation required.
- Electrical risk from wet equipment (AS/NZS 3760, LISTED).
- Manual handling of beds and trolleys.
- Patient-care risk: if equipment is moved or released without reprocessing or clinical sign-off, the risk is clinical, not only hygienic.

## 4. Regulator references

- Work health and safety: Safe Work Australia model laws; state regulators as in residential brief.
- Medical devices and reprocessing: Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care (ACSQHC) advisories (READ), and the Therapeutic Goods Administration (UNCONFIRMED this pass).
- Accreditation: facility accreditation bodies under the NSQHS Standards (READ via advisory).
- Health department guidance: state health departments (Victorian Department of Health PRIOR only).
- Overseas guidance (US CDC and FDA, Health Canada): PRIOR. Not Australian law.

## 5. Typical scope boundaries

- External cleaning only, under the facility's written instruction.
- No reprocessing, disinfection or sterilisation of reusable devices, and no internal or invasive device work.
- No release of equipment for patient use. The facility's clinical staff decide.
- Do not change facility processes or claim compliance with AS 5369 or NSQHS.
- Completing the course does not grant a licence, certification or authority to reprocess devices.
