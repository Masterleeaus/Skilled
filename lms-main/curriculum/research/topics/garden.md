## 1. Scope and boundaries

In scope:
- Weeding, light pruning, hedge trimming (low height, from the ground), leaf and debris clearance, and garden clean-ups.
- Mulching with approved products, and green waste sorting and removal.
- Hand tools and light powered garden tools, used within the tool's manufacturer limits.
- Client and site walk-through, scope agreement, and records.

Out of scope or referred:
- Tree felling, removal of large limbs, and work at height from ladders beyond the training limit. Refer to a qualified arborist (rules UNCONFIRMED per state in this pass).
- Herbicide or pesticide application for hire (see `lawn.md` section 5 for NSW).
- Moving soil, mulch, turf or potted plants out of fire ant biosecurity zones without the required steps (section 5).
- Disposal of asbestos or other hazardous debris (refer to a licensed removalist; section 5).
- Irrigation repairs, electrical and plumbing work, and structural landscaping.
- Use of elevated work platforms (see section 5 for the licence threshold).

## 2. Key technical methods

- Site walk-through: identify hazards (overhead lines, services, slopes, animals, nests, sharp waste, old structures) and agree the work area with the client.
- Hand pruning: cut at the correct point on the stem; remove dead and damaged wood first; do not remove more than the plant can carry (general horticulture; no standard cited).
- Hedge trimming: work from a stable footing; keep hands clear of blades; check the cord and RCD protection on electric trimmers.
- Leaf and debris clearance: rake and blow in a manner that avoids flying debris; keep bystanders clear.
- Mulching: use a mulch product that meets AS 4454 (composts, soil conditioners and mulches) per the listing below; state the depth and keep mulch clear of plant stems (depth UNCONFIRMED in this pass).
- Green waste: sort into compostable, woody, and contaminated (for example, weeds or asbestos suspected) streams; take to a council or licensed facility.
- Records: photos before and after, areas, tools used, products used, waste taken, and client sign-off.

## 4. Main hazards

- Falls from ladders and uneven ground.
- Powered tools: cutting injuries, kickback, thrown debris, noise and vibration, and cord damage near water.
- Manual handling of green waste bags, mulch bags and bins.
- Sharp waste and plant hazards: thorns, stings, allergens, and sap (specific hazards UNCONFIRMED in this pass).
- Heat, UV and dehydration.
- Asbestos in old soil, sheeting and debris. The garden clean-up crew should stop and refer when suspected material appears (thresholds in section 5).
- Biosecurity: spreading fire ants, weeds or diseases via soil, mulch, green waste or potted plants.
- Overhead and underground services (Before You Dig Australia check before any digging, read; no date).

## 5. Regulator references and licence facts

Height work:
- [NSW-16, CONFIRMED] WP high risk work licence needed for boom-type elevating work platforms with boom length 11 m or more. Window cleaning and similar tasks are not listed as classes.
- [NSW-17, CONFIRMED] SafeWork NSW working-at-heights page (modified 21 Oct 2026): construction SWMS where a fall of more than 2 m is possible; does not mention garden work. Whether it applies to garden work is UNCONFIRMED.
- [QLD-11, CONFIRMED] WP licence for boom-type platforms; platforms under 11 m excluded.
- [VIC-22, CONFIRMED] Scissor lifts do not need a high risk work licence (WorkSafe Victoria).
- [NT-21, CONFIRMED] Scissor lifts do not need a high risk work licence.
- [TAS-21, UNCONFIRMED] Scissor lift status not named on the classes page.
- [WA-19, UNCONFIRMED] Scissor lift class not identified.
- [ACT-23, UNCONFIRMED] Scissor lift not named.

Asbestos (garden debris and old structures):
- [QLD-10, CONFIRMED] Class A and Class B. No licence needed to remove 10 m2 or less of non-friable asbestos, but duties still apply (per matrix QLD notes). Homeowners removing more than 10 m2 need a certificate.
- [VIC-32, CONFIRMED] Unlicensed removal of non-friable asbestos: up to 10 m2 in total and 1 hour in any 7 days. OHS Regulations 2017.
- [NSW-15, PARTIAL] Class A and B names confirmed; thresholds UNCONFIRMED.
- [WA-26, CONFIRMED] Class B for more than 10 m2 of non-friable asbestos.
- [SA-20, CONFIRMED] Class B for more than 10 m2 of non-friable asbestos.
- [WA-27 and SA-21, UNCONFIRMED] Unlicensed allowance not stated in WA or SA pages read.

Biosecurity:
- Fire ant biosecurity zones (read, undated): soil, hay, mulch, manure, quarry materials, turf and potted plants have movement restrictions inside and outside Queensland. Anyone living or working in a zone who buys or sells these materials must follow the Biosecurity Regulation 2016 steps or apply for a biosecurity instrument permit. The fire ant page does not name garden waste or landscaping material specifically; treat them as UNCONFIRMED until checked with the regulator.

Pesticides and herbicides:
- [NSW pest management technician licence] Pesticides other than fumigants for a fee or reward (NSW Service page, 11 Feb 2026). Garden herbicide application for a fee is UNCONFIRMED under this licence.
- [NSW ground applicator licence] Weed spraying for a fee by individuals (EPA fact sheet, August 2018). Gardens and lawns are listed as amenity horticulture and do not need it.
- Other states: UNCONFIRMED in this pass.

Underground services:
- Before You Dig Australia (read; no date): free pre-excavation referral. Requests through an online portal with a free account.

Licences for general garden maintenance: none identified in the matrices. Record as UNCONFIRMED.

## 6. Scope points for licence notices

- The lesson does not authorise tree work, height work or pesticide application for hire.
- Where asbestos is suspected, stop work and refer; the lesson does not authorise removal.
- Where height work involves a boom platform at 11 m or more, the WP licence applies; the lesson does not grant it.
