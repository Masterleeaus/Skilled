## 1. Business scope

- One business: dog walking, including pet taxi lessons (DECISIONS.md).
- Pet sitting and house sitting are a separate course (petsit.md). Grooming is a separate course (groom.md).
- The course covers walks of collected dogs and dog transport between home and walk. Overnight care, boarding and minding in the sitter's or the owner's home are outside this course.

## 2. Regulatory position

Dog walking has no state licence in any jurisdiction read. Councils are the main regulators, and they act mainly on public land. Every council rule below is COUNCIL-SPECIFIC.

**State registration**
- VIC: CONFIRMED that dog walking is not one of the Domestic Animal Business types Agriculture Victoria lists. No state registration was found (DOG-VIC-01).
- NSW, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT, NT: UNCONFIRMED. No state dog-walking licence was found in the pages read (NAT-DOG-NT-01, NAT-DOG-SA-01, NAT-DOG-TAS-01, NAT-DOG-ACT-01, NAT-DOG-WA-01).
- ACT: CONFIRMED that the ACT pet business licensing page lists pet shops and businesses that board animals. Dog walking is not listed. This is not confirmed as an exemption. Confirm with the ACT Animal Welfare Authority (NAT-DOG-ACT-01).
- NT: the animal welfare page lists no licence for dog walking. This is an absence on one page, not a confirmed exemption (NAT-DOG-NT-01).
- TAS: the Dog Control Act FAQ names no licence for dog walking. Councils hold the local detail (NAT-DOG-TAS-01).

**Council permits for public land**
- VIC, Port Phillip: COUNCIL-SPECIFIC. A permit is needed when walking four or more dogs at a time. Limits: eight dogs per person and twelve in total. Dogs under effective control, leashes carried, council tags, records kept, dog attacks reported within 48 hours, permit carried while working (DOG-VIC-02). The fee dates on the page conflict and must be confirmed with the council.
- VIC, Yarra: COUNCIL-SPECIFIC. A permit is needed for a commercial enterprise exercising dogs on council land. Maximum four animals at one time. The fee and expiry date are on the council page (DOG-VIC-03). Public liability of at least 20 million dollars naming the council (DOG-VIC-04).
- NSW, Ryde: COUNCIL-SPECIFIC. A commercial minder permit for specified off-leash areas, valid one year. One commercial minder per permit. APRA-approved public liability of at least 10 million dollars. Up to four dogs. Annual fee band depends on whether the business is Ryde-based and on weekly hours. Activity hours are set by the form (DOG-NSW-01).
- NSW, Inner West and Lane Cove: UNCONFIRMED. A draft permit was deferred in 2023 (secondary news source, DOG-NSW-02). Current status not checked.
- QLD, Brisbane: UNCONFIRMED. The Brisbane dog permit page covers keeping dogs at home and says more than two dogs needs a permit. It does not address walking or minding businesses (NAT-DOG-QLD-01, DOG-QLD-01).

**Insurance**
- COUNCIL-SPECIFIC: Yarra and Ryde require public liability as above. Port Phillip's page does not mention insurance (DOG-VIC-04).
- No state-level public liability rule for dog walking was found (DOG-VIC-04).
- Workers compensation: CONFIRMED for employers in the states read. A contractor can be a deemed worker in some states (see hair.md section 2).

**Working with Children Check**
- VIC: CONFIRMED that the listed categories do not include pet care (DOG-VIC-07).

**Products and chemicals**
- UNCONFIRMED. No product or chemical rule found for dog walking (DOG-VIC-05). National model WHS duties apply to businesses in general (NAT-CHEM-01), but their application to dog walking is not confirmed.

**Walking on roads and home collection**
- UNCONFIRMED. The Victorian boarding code requires dogs walked on roads to be on a lead at all times, with the owner's written approval. That code is written for boarding establishments. Whether it reaches walkers who collect dogs from homes is UNCONFIRMED (DOG-VIC-06).

**Pet transport (pet taxi)**
- NSW: CONFIRMED that the NSW Animal Welfare Code of Practice No 1 covers anyone who transports companion animals, from collection through holding and transit to delivery. The page names no licence or registration. Vehicle, temperature and driver rules were not read. A pet taxi licence is UNCONFIRMED. The ABLIS copy carries a 2017 metadata date (NAT-TRANS-NSW-01).
- VIC: UNCONFIRMED. No pet-taxi licence or permit was found. The boarding code's driver responsibilities apply to boarding establishments. The livestock transport code is written for livestock (TRANS-VIC-01, TRANS-VIC-02).
- NT: CONFIRMED that dogs must be restrained when travelling in vehicles and trailers. The page names no pet-transport licence (NAT-TRANS-NT-01).
- ACT: CONFIRMED that the mandatory land transport code applies to livestock. It does not address companion animal transport (NAT-TRANS-ACT-01). Companion animal transport rules: UNCONFIRMED.
- QLD: UNCONFIRMED. Business Queensland's animal welfare codes list does not show a companion animal transport code. Absence on one page is not proof of no code (NAT-TRANS-QLD-01).
- SA, WA, TAS: UNCONFIRMED. Not checked in this pass, or only titles seen (NAT-TRANS-SA-01, NAT-TRANS-WA-01, NAT-TRANS-TAS-01).

**National animal welfare standards**
- The national standards and guidelines page names no standard for dogs, cats or companion animals. The national land transport of livestock standard was endorsed in 2012 and covers livestock. This does not establish that no national companion animal standard exists (agriculture.gov.au page checked 2026-11-02).

## 5. Scope boundaries and licence notices

- Dog walking on public land is council-specific. The lesson must name the council and say the permit does not transfer to other councils.
- Pet taxi is a lesson inside this course. Its legal position is UNCONFIRMED in most states. Each pet taxi lesson must say which states it has been checked for.
- Do not describe a permit, a completed course or an ACM30122 unit as an authority to walk dogs for hire. A council permit is a condition of council land use, not a qualification.
- Boarding is out of scope here. The Victorian boarding code and the NSW Code No 5 are for boarding establishments. They do not cover walking.
- Do not imply that Working with Children Check rules apply to dog walking in Victoria (DOG-VIC-07).
