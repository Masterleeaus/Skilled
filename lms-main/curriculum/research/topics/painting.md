## 1. Scope and boundaries

In scope:
- Interior and exterior painting of walls, ceilings, trim, doors, windows, fences and decks, on surfaces the course covers.
- Surface preparation: washing, scraping, sanding, filling, priming.
- Application by brush, roller and, where trained and equipped, spray.
- Colour, sheen and product selection from the label and the data sheet.
- Records, client protection, waste paint, and clean-up.

Out of scope or referred:
- Asbestos removal (licensed removalist; section 5). Painting over suspected asbestos is stopped and referred.
- Abrasive or heat removal of lead paint without the controls in AS/NZS 4361.2 (title listed; method per lesson). Refer to a specialist where the paint is unknown.
- Industrial painting, concrete floor and path painting, and signwriting in WA (exclusions listed in WA-01).
- Structural repairs, mould remediation (out of this course; see `pool.md` and `handyman.md` for related lesson placement), and roofing.
- Protective coatings in WA where the same contractor does the coating and a related process (exclusion note in WA-01).

## 2. Key technical methods

- Substrate check: identify timber, masonry, metal, plaster, fibre cement, or previously painted surface. Test previous coatings before sanding (lead and asbestos checks first; see section 5).
- Preparation: wash to remove dirt and chalk; scrape loose paint; sand to a key; fill; sand smooth; remove dust before priming.
- Priming: choose a primer compatible with the substrate and the topcoat; follow the label for coats and dry times.
- Application: work from the top down; keep a wet edge; apply the number of coats the label gives; avoid over-thinning.
- Spray work: only where trained and equipped; use overspray protection and respiratory protection as the SDS and label require.
- Curing and handover: allow the stated cure time; check for drips and runs; remove masking.
- Waste: store leftover paint and rags safely; disposal rules UNCONFIRMED per state.
- Records: surfaces, product names and batch numbers, coats, weather, and photos.

## 4. Main hazards

- Lead dust and fumes from disturbing old paint (AS/NZS 4361.2 title).
- Asbestos in older textured coatings, fillers, sheeting and adhesives (matrix thresholds in section 5).
- Solvent and VOC vapours; flammable liquids (SDS for each product; flammable storage UNCONFIRMED).
- Falls from ladders, step-ups and elevated work platforms (matrix thresholds in section 5).
- Slips on painted floors and drop sheets.
- Spray injection injuries from high-pressure equipment (UNCONFIRMED standard; treat as a high hazard when spray is used).
- Manual handling of ladders, drums and tins.
- Skin and eye contact with coatings and thinners.

## 5. Regulator references and licence facts

NSW:
- [NSW-01, CONFIRMED] Contractor licence (individual or company) or qualified supervisor certificate for painting work over $5,000 in labour and materials, including GST. Qualifications listed per trade (CPC30620 named). Page updated 23 June 2026.

QLD:
- [QLD-01, PARTIAL] QBCC class "Painting and decorating"; building work valued over $3,300 needs a licence. Class page last reviewed 31 August 2021, which conflicts with a 1 July 2026 fee note.

VIC:
- [VIC-01, CONFIRMED] Painting-only jobs are exempt from BPC registration (page updated 9 July 2025).
- [VIC-02, PARTIAL] Painting combined with another registrable skill (for example plastering) may need registration. Whether the $10,000 threshold also applies to combined jobs under that value is UNCONFIRMED.

WA:
- [WA-01, CONFIRMED] Painting contractor registration for painting work valued at $1,000 or more. The scope page (23 Dec 2025) says "or more"; a guide page (20 Dec 2024) says "over $1000". Conflict recorded in the matrix.
- [WA-02, CONFIRMED] Painting practitioner registration for supervising or working for a registered painting contractor.
- [WA-03, UNCONFIRMED] Work below $1,000 is not confirmed either way; do not read the $1,000 trigger as an exemption.

SA:
- [SA-01, PARTIAL] Painting is listed under domestic building work; building work contractor licence applies to a person carrying on business as a building work contractor; no dollar threshold stated (FAQ, November 2025).

TAS:
- [TAS-01, UNCONFIRMED] Trade class not confirmed; the category determination returned client errors on every fetch.

ACT:
- [ACT-01, PARTIAL] Painting is not among the ten construction occupations licensed under the Construction Occupations (Licensing) Act 2004 s 7; it is on a planned list of trades that may be licensed in future.

NT:
- [NT-01, UNCONFIRMED] No painting class on the Building Practitioners Board page read. Absence not confirmed as a rule.

Asbestos (affects painting, plastering, tiling and demolition):
- [VIC-32, CONFIRMED] Unlicensed removal of non-friable asbestos up to 10 m2 total and one hour in any seven days (OHS Regulations 2017). The unlicensed route gives no allowance for friable asbestos.
- [QLD-10, CONFIRMED] Class A (any friable; more than 10 m2 non-friable) and Class B (more than 10 m2 non-friable). Homeowners removing more than 10 m2 need a certificate.
- [NSW-15, PARTIAL] Class A and B names confirmed; thresholds UNCONFIRMED.
- [WA-25, WA-26, CONFIRMED] Class A and Class B (more than 10 m2 non-friable). [WA-27, UNCONFIRMED] Unlicensed allowance not stated.
- [SA-19, SA-20, CONFIRMED] Class A for friable and asbestos-backed material; Class B for more than 10 m2 non-friable. [SA-21, UNCONFIRMED] No unlicensed limit stated.
- [TAS-18, TAS-19, CONFIRMED] Class A and Class B; 10 m2 threshold for Class B (page updated 11 Nov 2025).
- [ACT-20, PARTIAL] Class A and Class B scope confirmed; thresholds not stated. [ACT-21, UNCONFIRMED] No licence-free threshold stated.
- [NT-19, CONFIRMED] Class A friable any amount; Class B non-friable more than 10 m2.

Height:
- Painting at height uses platforms, ladders and scaffolds. The WP licence applies to boom-type platforms at 11 m or more [NSW-16, QLD-11, WA-18, TAS-20, ACT-22, NT-20, CONFIRMED]. Scissor lifts: see `garden.md` section 5.

## 6. Scope points for licence notices

- NSW notice: contractor licence or qualified supervisor certificate for work over $5,000. The lesson does not grant it.
- QLD notice: QBCC painting and decorating class; threshold $3,300; class page date check pending.
- VIC notice: painting-only jobs are exempt from BPC registration; combined jobs may differ (UNCONFIRMED for the $10,000 cap).
- WA notice: painting contractor registration at $1,000 or more; do not state an exemption below that.
- The lesson does not grant a licence or a removal authority. Asbestos work is referred to a licensed removalist.

## 7. UNCONFIRMED points (summary)

1. Whether the VIC $10,000 cap applies to combined painting and plastering jobs.
2. Work below $1,000 in WA.
3. NSW asbestos thresholds (NSW-15).
4. Whether the QBCC painting class scope is current (2021 page date).
5. Painting licence status in TAS, NT and ACT (planned list).
6. Spray injection hazard standard and controls.
