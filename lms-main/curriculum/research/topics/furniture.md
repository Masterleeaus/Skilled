## 1. Scope and boundary

- Restoration of wooden and upholstered furniture: stripping, sanding, repair of joints and veneer, staining, French polishing, reupholstery and fabric renewal.
- Out of scope: structural engineering of antiques, museum conservation, and any work involving suspected hazardous finishes without a plan.
- Upholstery cleaning on a client's premises: the carpet and upholstery course.

## 2. Key methods and standards (Australia)

- Restoration method (stripping, sanding, finishing, joints, upholstery): craft and manufacturer practice. No Australian standard confirmed for furniture restoration. UNCONFIRMED.
- Lead in older paints and finishes: NSW Government LeadSmart renovation guidance (LISTED; search result title only, content not read). The guidance covers renovating older homes and lead paint. Its application to furniture: UNCONFIRMED.
- Paint and finish removal chemicals (strippers, solvents): SDS duties. The Safe Work Australia SDS page is PRIOR only.
- Wood dust from sanding: WorkSafe Victoria wood dust guidance (READ, reviewed 17/04/2026). Dust Class M extraction; avoid compressed air; respirator controls. See timber brief.
- Foam and upholstery fire safety: UNCONFIRMED. Do not state a standard without a source.
- Electrical equipment (sanders, heat guns, steamers): AS/NZS 3760 (LISTED).
- Hot-air and heat guns: manufacturer instructions. UNCONFIRMED standard.

## 3. Main hazards

- Lead in older paint and finishes when sanded or heat-stripped. Stop and test where suspected.
- Solvent and stripper exposure. Ventilation and PPE per SDS.
- Wood dust from sanding (see timber brief).
- Fire risk from solvent-soaked rags, heat guns and oil-based finishes: UNCONFIRMED specific guidance.
- Manual handling of heavy furniture.
- Asbestos in old upholstery backings, adhesives or insulation: stop and refer (asbestos code, READ for model status only).
- Pests and mould in old furniture: out of scope; High-risk module if needed.

## 5. Typical scope boundaries

- Do not sand or heat-strip old finishes without first testing for lead where the age or source suggests it. Stop and refer if the test is positive or unknown.
- No restoration of upholstery furniture that may contain asbestos without a clearance or specialist plan.
- Do not claim heritage, value or authenticity outcomes.
- Completing the course does not grant any licence.
