# PV wording proposal: shared lessons carrying the PV licence line

Status: proposal only. No lesson file has been changed, committed or staged. Repository root: `/home/claude/p29m/lms-main`.

## Scope and method

- Searched `curriculum/courses` and `curriculum/shared` for the exact line `Whether PV panel cleaning or PV isolation needs a specific licence has not yet been confirmed`. Found 54 lesson files, all under `curriculum/shared/`. None under `curriculum/courses/` carries the exact line.
- Each of the 54 files carries the sentence twice: once in the `Electrical notice` instruction block and once in the `safetyAndRegulatory` point. The proposed sentence replaces the whole current sentence in both places.
- Each lesson's own work was read from its title, learning outcomes, procedure, instruction and knowledge checks. The shared `Electrical notice` paragraph and the `solar panel cleaning track` context line were excluded from the work-type judgement.

## Work types found

- **Cleaning-track generic lesson: no PV task of its own** (48 lessons). Chemicals, induction, job management, quality, safety and customer modules. None washes panels or isolates, tests or repairs a PV system. The solar track is only the course context.
- **Portable electrical equipment isolation, testing or repair, not PV** (6 lessons). These isolate or tag out portable equipment. None works on a PV system.
- **Panel-cleaning-only lessons** (0 lessons). No shared lesson performs panel washing as its own task.
- **PV isolation, testing or repair lessons** (0 lessons). No shared lesson performs PV work as its own task.

## Proposed sentences

Form A (cleaning-track generic, 48 lessons):

> On the Victorian, Western Australian and New South Wales pages checked, plain panel washing is not stated to need a licence, and any electrical work on a PV array is referred to a licensed electrician.

Form B (portable equipment isolation, 6 lessons):

> Isolation and testing in this lesson apply to portable equipment only, not to a PV system. Electrical testing, isolation and repair of PV systems need a licensed electrician; in Victoria, Energy Safe Victoria guidance supports installation, maintenance and repair by licensed electrical workers, but not testing or who may isolate.

## Decisions made (for review)

1. Used a third form for the 48 generic lessons. The user's two forms do not fit them, because none of them does panel washing or PV work as its own task. The generic lessons get the panel-washing form, since they sit on the solar cleaning track.
2. Portable-equipment isolation lessons get a separate sentence. Their isolation is of portable equipment, so the PV licence wording must say so rather than imply PV work.
3. Dropped the 'Queensland and Western Australia only partly checked' clause. The decided position names Victoria, WA and NSW only, so the proposal states those three states and makes no QLD claim.
4. Kept the existing 'Electrical notice' paragraph around the sentence unchanged. Its first sentences already say the lesson does not authorise work on, isolation of or repair to PV systems.
5. Kept the Victorian limit only in Form B, where isolation and testing are in scope. Form A needs no Victorian limit, as it makes no testing or isolation claim.

## Not covered

- 30 course JSON files mention PV or solar alongside an unconfirmed status, and not all of them concern licences. Several carry different licence wording, such as 'Licence rules for PV cleaning have not yet been confirmed' (for example `curriculum/courses/solar-panel-cleaning/solar-panel-cleaning-L07.json`). They do not carry the exact line, so they are outside this proposal and need a separate pass.
- The `curriculum/courses/` REPORT files and `curriculum/research/UNCONFIRMED-INVENTORY.md` were not edited.
