## 1. Method and status key

- Every NSW and QLD source URL in the earlier matrix was fetched on 2026-11-02. Pages not fetched are marked as such.
- CONFIRMED: stated on an official page read in this pass.
- PARTIAL: part of the answer is on an official page; the gap is noted in the row.
- UNCONFIRMED: no official source found, or the source is silent. "Not identified" means not found, not "does not exist".
- Sources used: nsw.gov.au (Building Commission NSW pages), SafeWork NSW, NSW Police SLED, NSW Planning Portal and planning.nsw.gov.au, fire.nsw.gov.au (NSW Fire and Rescue), QBCC, WorkSafe Queensland, qld.gov.au Fair Trading, and the QBCC fence factsheet.
- Not consulted in this pass: NSW Fair Trading as a separate source (NSW building licences are on Building Commission NSW pages), the Office of Industrial Relations, and council pages for Queensland decks and fences (see gaps).

## 3. Key findings by trade

- Painting and plastering (NSW): contractor licence or qualified supervisor certificate for work over $5,000 (labour and materials, including GST). Dry plastering only; wet plastering and rendering not covered on the page.
- Painting and plastering (QLD): QBCC classes exist. Building work over $3,300 needs a licence (QBCC threshold). The class pages date from 2021.
- Tiling: NSW wall and floor tiling page does not name floating floors. QLD wall and floor tiling class does not name them either. Floating floors remain UNCONFIRMED in both states.
- Carpentry, decks and fences: NSW carpentry page names decks and fencing only in the footing context. QLD fence factsheet requires a licence (Carpentry for timber and metal fences) with a $3,300 threshold; decks not named.
- Locksmith: NSW individual locksmiths need Class 2C (SLED). QLD needs a security equipment installer licence (exemptions as listed in QLD-04). NSW company licence: UNCONFIRMED.
- Plumbing and gas: NSW licence for any cost, with named categories including irrigation. QLD plumbing, drainage and gas fitting at any value (QBCC).
- Electrical and solar: NSW requires electrical contractor licence holders for wiring and a building or electrical contractor licence to contract for solar installs. NSW solar page does not cover cleaning or isolation. QLD electrical mechanic licence for work on energy generation and storage systems; PV not named; cleaning and isolation UNCONFIRMED in both states.
- Pool: NSW owner duties (register, certificate of compliance, barrier design) and minor barrier repairs up to $1,000 by endorsed contractor or qualified supervisor certificate holders. QLD PSI licence for inspection and minor barrier repairs; QBCC pool maintenance class for maintenance. Chemical servicing licences: UNCONFIRMED in both states.
- Height work: WP licence for boom-type platforms only (NSW 11 m or more; QLD platforms under 11 m excluded). Window cleaning, pressure cleaning and scissor lifts are not named in either state. Roof and solar cleaning: UNCONFIRMED.
- Asbestos: NSW Class A and B names confirmed, thresholds not on the page. QLD Class A and B with the 10 m2 non-friable threshold; homeowners removing over 10 m2 need a certificate.

## 4. Planning rules (where they affect licensing)

- NSW decks (Planning Portal, updated 23 March 2026): exempt limits include 3 m maximum height above existing ground, 900 mm or 5 m setback depending on zone, 25 m2 maximum area.
- NSW fences (Planning Portal, updated 20/06/2023): residential zones R1-R4 and RU5: side and rear fences up to 1.8 m (1.2 m if masonry); road frontage fences up to 1.2 m.
- Licence effect (analysis, not a source statement): planning exemption does not change the NSW $5,000 threshold or the QLD $3,300 threshold. A deck or fence that is exempt from planning can still need a licence.
- QLD: state deck and fence planning rules not found. QBCC pool fences page says some fences need building approval and to check with the council before building a fence higher than 2 m.

## 7. Implications for the Titan Academy licence rule

Under the DECISIONS.md licence rule, lessons covering licensed work need a licence notice that says what the licence covers and that the lesson does not grant it. Suggested handling from this pass:

- Locksmith lessons: NSW Class 2C (individual) and QLD security equipment installer, state-specific notices only. Do not state a Victorian locksmith licence position.
- Height and roof cleaning: WP notice should say the licence covers boom-type platforms only (NSW 11 m or more; QLD under 11 m excluded). Say that window and pressure cleaning are not named classes in either state.
- Electrical near solar: notice should say PV wiring and maintenance are electrical work for licensed electrical workers in NSW and QLD. Do not name which licence class covers isolation or cleaning (UNCONFIRMED in both).
- Pool chemicals: NSW (no licence found) and QLD (retailer notification threshold for more than 2,500 L hypochlorite) can be cited as source notes. Do not state that a licence is or is not needed for pool servicing.
- Deck and fence: NSW lessons should note the $5,000 contractor licence threshold and the exempt planning limits; QLD lessons should note the fence licence and $3,300 threshold, and that deck licensing is not confirmed.
- Asbestos: quote the QLD 10 m2 limit only with its source (WorkSafe Queensland). Do not generalise it to NSW.
