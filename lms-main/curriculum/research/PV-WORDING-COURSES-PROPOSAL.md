# PV wording proposal: course lessons with PV licence wording worded differently

Status: proposal only. No lesson file has been changed, committed or staged. Repository root: `/home/claude/p29m/lms-main`. Companion to `curriculum/research/PV-WORDING-PROPOSAL.md`, which covers the 54 shared lessons under `curriculum/shared/`.

## Scope and method

- Every string value in every lesson JSON under `curriculum/courses/` was read (parsed, not grepped), split into sentences, and tested for a PV term (PV, photovoltaic, solar) in the same sentence as an unconfirmed-status phrase (for example "not yet been confirmed", "UNCONFIRMED", "could not be read").
- 21 lesson files matched. Three were then excluded (see "Not changed") and 18 are proposed here.
- Source-list and reference lines (`sourceReferences`, `referencesAtStart`, `referencesAtEnd`, `officialReferences`, and "UNCONFIRMED:" summary lines) were not proposed for change. They are reference metadata, not the licence line.
- Sentences that are unconfirmed for reasons other than licence status (AS/NZS text, inverter switch-off, warranty, water quality, wildlife) were not changed.
- Each proposed sentence keeps the shared position: plain panel washing is not stated to need a licence on the Victorian, Western Australian and New South Wales pages checked; electrical testing, isolation and repair of PV systems need a licensed electrician; in Victoria, the limit is as the Energy Safe Victoria page states, with testing and who may isolate not confirmed for Victoria.

## Work types

- **A. Panel cleaning only.** The lesson's own work is washing or preparing to wash PV panels (booking and access, cleaning method, high-pressure use, runoff).
- **B. Electrical isolation, testing or repair.** The lesson's own work touches isolating, testing or repairing PV electrical parts, or deciding when to refer them.
- **R. Records and referral only.** A PV cleaning lesson whose own work is recording defects, handover and escalation. It does no cleaning or electrical work itself.
- **G. Generic lesson, no PV task of its own.** Window, pressure or roof soft-washing lessons. The PV line is a boundary note only.

## Decisions for review

- D1. Every panel-washing line keeps "other states and territories are not covered by that statement". The current lines say other states are not confirmed. Dropping that would overstate the position, so it was kept.
- D2. Victorian guidance is attributed only where the lesson cites the Energy Safe Victoria "Safely installing photovoltaic (PV) systems" page, which the lesson sources show as read (roof L05, solar L02). Pressure, window and some solar lessons cite the Energy Safe Victoria PV installer safety guideline, which the lesson itself says could not be read. Those lines say "could not be read" and do not attribute the other page's content to Victoria.
- D3. Electrical testing, isolation and repair needing a licensed electrician is taken from the shared position. It is not separately sourced per state in these files. Victoria is supported by the Energy Safe Victoria page as read. New South Wales repair is supported by the licence page cited in solar L05 and L11. The Queensland and Western Australian sentences were not re-verified here.
- D4. Queensland and other states are not listed as checked. Current wording says "not confirmed", so the proposals say the same.
- D5. Solar L10 first sentence is split. The wastewater clause stays; the licence clause moves to row 28 so it is not repeated.
- D6. Non-PV unconfirmed statuses in the window, pressure and roof soft-washing lessons (window, pressure and roof soft-washing licences) are kept in the rewritten sentences. Only the PV part changes.
- D7. Type B was given to solar L02 and roof L05 because each lesson's own work is about isolation at the switchboard and what to refer. They are not cleaning lessons. Solar L02 is B and not A.
- D8. Type R (records only) was used for solar L11 and L12, which do no cleaning or electrical work of their own.
- D9. Rows 4 and 7, and rows 9 and 11, overlap. Rows 22 and 21, and 29 and 31, are the same sentence in two places within one file. Each is listed so that every current sentence has a replacement.
- D10. The phrase "could not be read in this pass" is removed from learner text. The replacements say "could not be read" instead, which states the same fact without the review-process wording.
- D11. The 21-to-18 count gap is explained above. The brief expected about 30 files. The 21 matches were the full set of lesson files with a PV term and an unconfirmed status in one sentence. Nine more files mention solar only in a "does not mention" source note and are not counted.
