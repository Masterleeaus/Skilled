# PV wording applied (item 4)
Applied `curriculum/research/PV-WORDING-PROPOSAL.md` to the 54 shared lessons, as written. In each lesson the sentence below was replaced in both places it appears (the `Electrical notice` instruction block and the `safetyAndRegulatory` point), 2 replacements per file, 108 in total. Each file was parsed as JSON after the edit. No options or answer keys were changed. Nothing was committed.
Old sentence (replaced in every file):
> Whether PV panel cleaning or PV isolation needs a specific licence has not yet been confirmed in every state and territory checked (Queensland and Western Australia are only partly checked on this point).
Form A new sentence (48 cleaning-track generic lessons):
> On the Victorian, Western Australian and New South Wales pages checked, plain panel washing is not stated to need a licence, and any electrical work on a PV array is referred to a licensed electrician.
Form B new sentence (6 portable-equipment lessons):
> Isolation and testing in this lesson apply to portable equipment only, not to a PV system. Electrical testing, isolation and repair of PV systems need a licensed electrician; in Victoria, Energy Safe Victoria guidance supports installation, maintenance and repair by licensed electrical workers, but not testing or who may isolate.
